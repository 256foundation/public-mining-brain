## Software Specifications (verbatim claims)

- Same stack as AT2880: Power Management (QSE/Voltus grid integration), EnergyTune™ (ECO/Turbo via DVFS), AutoTemp, Hardware-Based Secure Boot ("hardware-based root of trust"), APIs with Foreman/AwesomeMiner, FluxVision™ (ZTP, miner groups).
- Extra FluxVision feature on this sheet: **FluxGPT** — "the generative AI-based conversational experience that's designed to assist you in finding answers or information on Auradine products quickly and efficiently."

## Fleet/commercial posture

- Immersion-only warranty restricted to two named fluids (Engineered Fluids Bitcool 888, Shell S3X) — relevant for deployment planning.
- 7.5 kW PSU class; no pricing (fleet/commercial sales).
