## Launch summary (verbatim)

"Canaan Inc. (NASDAQ: CAN), the global powerhouse in high-performance Bitcoin mining hardware, successfully debuted its groundbreaking Avalon A16 series (A16XP-300T, 12.8J/TH ±5%; A16-282T, 13.8J/TH ±5%) miner this week at the Blockchain Life 2025 event held in Dubai Festival City. The unveiling confirmed the arrival of Canaan's most competitive model to date, promising a significant leap in efficiency, stability, and hash rate capability for large-scale mining operations globally."

"The centerpiece of Canaan's presence at Booth L15, the Avalon A16XP immediately captured industry attention with its powerful Hashrate of 300 TH/s."

## Key Specifications Confirmed for the Avalon A16XP (verbatim block)

- Product Model: A16XP
- Encryption Algorithm/Monetary Unit: SHA256/BTC
- Hash Rate: 300 TH/s (with a tolerance of ±5% at 25°C)
- Design Focus: "Powerful Hashrate, Global Compatibility, and Improved Power Efficiency"
- AC Voltage Input: 220~277 Volts
- Working Temperature Range: -5 to 35°C
- Net Dimensions: 366mm (L) * 213mm (W) * 300mm (H)

## Engineering claims (verbatim)

- "the Avalon A16XP was outfitted with an all-new fan array and wind-tunnel design. This innovative hardware configuration allows the unit to run markedly quieter while simultaneously inheriting Avalon's renowned tolerance for extreme ambient temperatures. This battle-tested resilience ensures unwavering stability, whether operating in desert heat or alpine cold."
- Keynote at Blockchain Life 2025 by Dr. Jinghao Chen, VP of Canaan: "Bitcoin Mining for the Future of Energy & The New Miners" (Oct 28).
- Positioning: "enabling Bitcoin mining to stabilize grids, monetize renewables, and unlock new economic models for clean-power generation."
