## Series narrative (verbatim)

- "The new generation of Avalon Miner — Most powerful chip ever."
- "One of the biggest highlights of the A14 series. As one of the most advanced chip manufacturing technologies, while the size of the chip has become smaller, miners' performance and energy efficiency have made a qualitative leap. This makes the Avalon A14 series one of the most powerful computing solutions on the market."
- "In addition to high performance and energy efficiency, the A14 series has also changed the traditional chip manufacturing model, allowing miners to adapt to more scenarios. Avalon A14 can operate stably and provide reliable computing power in desert heat, ice, snow, or other extreme environments."
- "Earn more for less."

## Immersion claims (verbatim; A14 series includes immersion variants)

- "Noiseless and Eco-friendly — The individual modules of the immersion cooling miners operate seamlessly in liquid coolant, resulting in a noise-free mining experience. Reducing the heat emitted by the hardware helps improve the operating environment of the mining farm, thereby saving on cooling costs."
- "Superior Durability — The mining module is fully immersed in liquid coolant, eliminating any possibility of dust absorption within the miner. This helps reduce static damage caused by fine dust particles inside, extending the life of miners."
