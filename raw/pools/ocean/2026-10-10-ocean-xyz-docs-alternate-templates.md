# OCEAN docs — Alternate Templates

> Source: https://ocean.xyz/docs/templateselection
> Collected: 2026-10-10
> Published: 2023-12-20
> Registry ID: ocean-pool

---

OCEAN formerly offered alternate block template options for miners who desired different policies than the OCEAN default, before DATUM made it possible for miners to control their own block templates. The alternate template information below is provided for miners looking for a starting point to configure their own block templates.

- **OCEAN Recommended (default, 2% fee)**
- mine.ocean.xyz:3334 or default.mine.ocean.xyz:3101
- Includes only transactions and reasonably small data.

We decommissioned our alternate template options on December 21st, 2025.

- **Core+Antispam (2% fee)**

- **Core (2% fee)**

- **Data-Free (2% fee)**
