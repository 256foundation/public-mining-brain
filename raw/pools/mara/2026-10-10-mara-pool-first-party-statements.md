## From "DMG Blockchain Set To Join Marathon's Bitcoin Mining Pool, MaraPool" (Sept 9, 2021)

- **Who runs it:** Marathon Digital Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ:MARA), "one of the largest enterprise Bitcoin self-mining companies in North America."
- **Who can join:** opened to external Bitcoin mining companies — DMG Blockchain became "the first North American Bitcoin miner, excluding Marathon, to join Marathon's mining pool, MaraPool," pointing 100% of its hashrate to the pool from Sept 13, 2021. CEO Fred Thiel: "After experiencing difficulties with mining pools outside the U.S., we decided to launch our own pool that would reflect the values and needs of enterprising North American Bitcoin miners… we have now opened the pool to other Bitcoin miners that share our beliefs."
- **Positioning:** "MaraPool is a U.S.-based Bitcoin mining pool… focused on reducing the environmental impact of Bitcoin mining through carbon neutral mining."
- **Audit/transparency:** "The pool, which is **fully audited by a third-party firm in the U.S.**, provides its members with industry leading transparency, an intuitive reporting system, and access to **exclusive services from NYDIG**" (NYDIG = institutional bitcoin custody/services — the custody-adjacent member benefit disclosed).
- **Custody flavor:** compliance-oriented, US-centric pool; DMG's CEO contrasted it with pools "lack[ing] transparency and accountability." (Context: Marathon had earlier in 2021 announced a "fully compliant" pool filtering OFAC-sanctioned transactions — widely reported and controversial; the PR itself does not restate the OFAC filtering.)
- Join path at the time: "To learn more about MaraPool, or to apply to join, visit marapool.com."

## From "MARA Announces Bitcoin Production and Mining Operation Updates for March 2025" (April 3, 2025)

CEO Fred Thiel (first-party fee/custody posture):

- "**MARAPool is the only self-owned and operated mining pool among public miners**, offering distinctive control and efficiency. **Operating our own pool means no fees to external operators**, higher earnings and superior performance."
- "MARA Pool's luck factor has exceeded the network average by over 10% since launch, meaning more blocks mined and higher rewards."
- March 2025 operating stats (MARAPool only, excludes joint ventures): 242 blocks won (+17% m/m), 829 BTC produced, share of available miner rewards 5.8%, transaction fees 1.3% of total, energized hashrate 54.3 EH/s.

## Fee / payout / custody summary (from these first-party sources)

- **Fee:** "no fees to external operators" is framed as the benefit of self-operation (from the miner's perspective, MARA pays no external pool fee; whether external members are charged is **not stated** — numeric fee not disclosed).
- **Payout scheme/threshold/cadence:** not disclosed in either document.
- **Custody/who can join:** application-based membership (2021) for miners "that share our beliefs"; member services via NYDIG; pool operator is a US public company (NASDAQ: MARA) with third-party US audit. Current membership path unpublished (pool site down).
- **SV2/DATUM:** not mentioned in either document.
- **Incidents/status:** none in these documents; mempool.space data in this repo (2026-10-10) shows MARA Pool at rank 6 with 50 blocks in the trailing week.
