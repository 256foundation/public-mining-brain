You can now split Bitcoin mining rewards with no pool operator.

Solo-style template control, with much lower payout variance.

One-click on Umbrel and StartOS. Install, paste a payout address, it joins the GridPool network.

No custodial wallet. No sharechain. No central share accountant. You keep your own templates hidden.

SV2 only for this beta. If you run Umbrel or Start9 with SV2 firmware, I need you on the network.

Packages:

StartOS: https://github.com/gridlabs-science/gridpool-startos/releases/tag/v0.2.2-beta.7

Umbrel: https://github.com/gridlabs-science/gridpool-umbrel/releases/tag/v0.2.2-beta.9

Main package, for other server setups: https://github.com/gridlabs-science/boot-protocol/releases
