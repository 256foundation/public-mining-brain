Yesterday I was able to get Hydrapool running as a native Umbrel app, and after a lot of debugging I finally got it installed, connected, and began mining with a Bitaxe.

Would love to contribute to this and other projects, just want to ensure that I’m not inadvertently stepping on any toes in the process.
