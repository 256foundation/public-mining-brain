Hi OSMU, Carlos from Power CM Software here. We also operate BTC PoW Lab Pool, which is listed and featured on this site, so I want to make that relationship explicit.

We built Find Mining Pools to put pool reward models, fees, registration requirements and payout rules next to their sources: https://findminingpools.com/

The directory currently has 22 profiles. You can filter by reward model, select pools for comparison and open the details behind each profile. Missing information stays marked as not reported rather than being treated as zero.

For a small miner, the useful question is often more specific than the headline fee: what portion goes to the finder, what is shared, and which payout conditions apply? The site is a starting point for checking those rules against the operator documentation, not an audit or a guarantee of returns.

Pool operators can submit a free editorial listing and documented corrections. Paid promotional placements are separate from the directory rankings.
