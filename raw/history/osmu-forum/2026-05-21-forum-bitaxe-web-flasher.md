## What Is the Bitaxe Web Flasher?

The Bitaxe Web Flasher was created to make firmware management for Bitaxe devices simple, fast, and accessible. [Video Guide](https://youtu.be/3er_m3V8798)

Instead of downloading command-line tools or setting up complex environments, users can flash, update, recover, and manage supported Bitaxe firmware directly from their browser.

The goal was straightforward:

- Make firmware flashing easier
- Reduce setup friction for new users
- Improve recovery workflows
- Support rapid development and testing
- Help the community stay updated with the latest releases

Whether you’re setting up your first Bitaxe or developing custom firmware builds, the Web Flasher is designed to streamline the process.
