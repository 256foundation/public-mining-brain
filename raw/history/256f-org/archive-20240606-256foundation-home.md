The 256 Foundation
          

A Free Open Source Bitcoin Mining, Energy, & Education Initiative.

The 256 Foundation is a 501(c)(3) public charity, all donations are tax deductable.


We educate individuals, organizations, and businesses about intelligent energy use and variable load controllers through Free and Open Source development. We support educators and developers with grants while they work to advance secure and sustainable energy initiatives; at our mining lab we equip developers with resources for continued success. We believe in FOSS development and we pledge that every project from this foundation will always be made available through FOSS and public domain contributions.

This foundation was formed in February 2024 by Rod [@bitkite](https://twitter.com/bitkite) and [@econoalchemist](https://twitter.com/econoalchemist).
