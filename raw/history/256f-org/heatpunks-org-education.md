## MORE RESOURCES IN DEVELOPMENT

We're building the educational foundation for the hashrate heating industry. Tutorials, technical guides, installation documentation, safety standards — this content doesn't exist yet because we're the ones creating it.

Want to help build this industry? Our parent nonprofit, the 256 Foundation, funds open-source Bitcoin mining and mining decentralization - and hashrate heating fits squarely within that mission, including the educational content, documentation, and training this space needs. The Foundation funds open-source work only, so anything a grant produces - code, docs, or education - must be released publicly.

Support this work — [Donations to the 256 Foundation](https://www.256foundation.org/donate) fund free educational content for the hashrate heating industry.
