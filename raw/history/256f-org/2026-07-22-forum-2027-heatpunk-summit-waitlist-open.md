The 2027 Heatpunk Summit waitlist is now open. Make sure to secure your spot!

https://www.heatpunks.org/summit

The Heatpunk Summit is a two-day developer-friendly event - focused on bringing together the bitcoin mining and building industries. 

If you're working on a hashrate heating product, firmware, control, education, integration or anything else - this is a great event for 256 Foundation contributors to attend.

This year's summit will feature workshops centered around Mujina, Ember One, Libre Board and Hydrapool.
