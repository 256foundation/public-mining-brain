## WHY ATTEND

Three years. Three chapters. Each summit built on the last.

### The First Gathering

The first Heatpunk Summit brought together mining developers, pleb builders, tinkerers, firmware hackers, and heating and building experts for the first time ever. Around 150 people in the same room who had never met. The takeaway: we needed to get organized. That gathering led directly to heatpunks.org, the community forum, and everything since.

### Formalizing the Movement

The second summit showed what organization looks like. More polished demo systems. More expertise. Professionals from ASHRAE and the DOE. Mining firmware developers. Building engineers. And Hashrate Heatpunks became a formal community project of the 256 Foundation — the 501(c)(3) building the open-source mining stack — with summit host Tyler Stevens named president of its board, giving the community a real organizational home.

### No More Barriers. What Will You Build?

The open-source mining stack is in the wild — firmware, hashboard and control-board reference designs, and pool implementations, all open. The one thing that always held builders back is gone. Year three is about acceleration: polish, possibility, and what comes next. Come ready to show what you've built, dig into the public data, and figure out what to tackle now that the barriers are down.

## THE BARRIERS ARE GONE

For years, one thing blocked builders more than anything else: closed mining firmware. That barrier is gone. The 256 Foundation has released the open-source mining stack into the wild — and paired with modern AI coding tools, there has never been less standing between an idea and a working build.

[> Open-source firmwareOPEN ↗The historic barrier. Closed mining firmware locked builders out — now it runs free on real hardware, hacked onto miners by heatpunks everywhere.](https://github.com/256foundation)

[> Hashboard reference designsOPEN ↗Open schematics and layouts for the boards that do the work. Fork them, spin your own, build for heat.](https://github.com/256foundation)

[> Control board reference designsOPEN ↗The brains of the machine, documented and open. No reverse-engineering required.](https://github.com/256foundation)

[> Mining pool implementationsOPEN ↗Open, documented pool software. Point your hashrate where you want it — no permission needed.](https://github.com/256foundation)

So what will you build?

### AND THE DATA IS PUBLIC

It is no longer just firmware and hardware. The last year produced real, public evidence — case studies, research papers, policy briefs, and build playbooks, all out in the open. HPS 2027 puts that record on the table and asks the questions that move the industry forward: **What does the data actually show? What is still missing** for residential, commercial, and regulatory adoption? Whether you build systems, model buildings, or write policy — this is where the numbers get pressure-tested.

Where the open-source mining stack stands, real build data, case studies, and the policy and regulatory picture. Real numbers and real builds — not pitch decks.
