Immersion vs hydro cooled shouldn’t matter of the radiant system. Either should be considered a “boiler” in the setup.

With that being said, I’m going with a hydro heatCore system so I don’t have to deal with immersion fluid. It sounds like you might have a non-residential setup where adding an immersion tank might not create additional overhead.
