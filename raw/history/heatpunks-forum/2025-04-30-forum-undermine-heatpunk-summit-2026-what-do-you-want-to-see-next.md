JimboTheMaxi | 2025-05-05 17:42:57 UTC | #4

Topics Worth Exploring:

Bridging the Hardware Gap:
How do we get from industrial immersion and hydro miners to home-scale hash-heat systems? The 3-phase power bottleneck is real. What’s needed to make this tech residential-ready?

The Secret Sauce of Site Selection:
What truly makes a mining + heating location ideal? Beyond energy cost, consider the mix: green energy vs. waste heat, existing infrastructure, and orange-pilled decision-makers. Are there specific KPIs that help identify addressable and scalable opportunities?

Revenue-Sharing Models for Residential Hash-Heat:
What’s the optimal structure when a homeowner is heating their home with a Bitcoin miner they don’t fully own?

Three emerging models worth unpacking:

- Full Ownership: Homeowner/client  buys the hardware, pays for electricity, and keeps all the BTC. Simple, but requires upfront capital and technical know-how.

- Hybrid Model: The Company owns and services the miner. Homeowner pays for heat and maintenance, while earning BTC-based rebates or sharing in the mining rewards. Aligns incentives and lowers barriers to entry.

- Heater-as-a-Service: The unit is treated like a standard heating appliance. The homeowner pays a flat monthly fee for heat, with no exposure to mining or BTC—just lower costs driven by a smarter backend.

Each model balances capital expenditure, risk, and user experience differently. Which one wins may depend on regulation, grid integration, and how “Bitcoin-native” the homeowner/client base becomes.

Each structure has implications for compliance, cash flow, and customer alignment.

Mining Pools & Block Template Strategy for Residential Miners:
As block template wars heat up, how should hash-heat systems engage? Should there be a standard? What block template policies or pool behaviors matter most for this corner of the industry?

You’re Not Bullish Enough:
The heating industry represents a multi-billion-dollar TAM. Most Bitcoiners still think in terms of power plants and containers, but every water heater and boiler is a future mining node waiting to be plugged in.
