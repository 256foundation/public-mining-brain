tylerkstevens | 2025-08-18 20:23:11 UTC | #4

There aren’t a lot of plug and play solutions out there if you want something more integrated than say, a space heater. It’s totally possible. But what I’ve found is that the people building the mining heaters don’t do the other typical steps of any heating business:

1. Sizing → 2. Product Recommendation → 3. Installation

When it’s not a space heater you plug in yourself, you need help with step 1 and 3.

Long story short, I’m building my company, [Exergy](https://exergyheat.com), to solve that problem. Go ahead and shoot us and email and we can help you weigh options. [contact@exergyheat.com](mailto:contact@exergyheat.com)
