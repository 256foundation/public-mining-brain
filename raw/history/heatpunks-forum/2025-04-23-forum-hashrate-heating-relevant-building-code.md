Howdy Everyone,

We are all getting to the point now where, to scale appropriately, we need to meet certain portions of the building codes. I have a few projects now that are getting inspected and we are on-target to full compliance.

I am going to use this post, and update this section, to list relevant portions of the International Building Code. I will also do another post underneath relevant to my state of Idaho.

If your state has its own unique codes that you think are relevant, go ahead and post it in this thread.

All IBC:  [https://codes.iccsafe.org/codes/i-codes](https://codes.iccsafe.org/codes/i-codes)

Residential Code: [https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2024P2](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IRC2024P2)

Plumbing Code: [https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IPC2024P1](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IPC2024P1)

Mechanical Code: [https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IMC2024V1.0](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IMC2024V1.0)

Swimming Pools and Spa Code: [https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/ISPSC2024V1.0](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/ISPSC2024V1.0)

Existing Building Code: [https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IEBC2024V1.0](https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/IEBC2024V1.0)
