You could get one of those small insulated tanks from any home supply store. Instead of getting a specialty tank, just put a cheap pump on the water heater tank output and connect it to a temperature controlled relay. Run it through a brazed plate HX and back to the tank. You may need a check valve or two but I think it would work.
