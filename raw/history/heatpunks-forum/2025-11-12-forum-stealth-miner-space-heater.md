I thought I’d share the Stealth Miner build I did for my parents. They have a nat gas furnace but use an electric heater for spot heating. The Stealth Miner build follows SatStackingPleb’s design. Using the ewelink app with the Thermo Plug-in pilot feature, Sonoff zigbee bridge hub, Sonoff temp sensor SNZB-02D and Sonoff S40 smart plug. Set to 80% fan getting about 600 watts with 20 TH/s. Hopefully this helps someone starting out. The controls are about $60 and not that difficult to setup. My Dad uses his cellphone to adjust temp and enable/disable the miner.

Go build stuff and stack sats!
