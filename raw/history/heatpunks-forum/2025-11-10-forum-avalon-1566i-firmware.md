They have high performance mode normal and power level mode. The power level mode has you entering a number to overclock or under clock but I’m just not sure how it works. I put both machines on power level 4 and one will run at 170t and the other will be at 200t. I think it has to do with temperature because Ive noticed the machine closer to the injection side on the C2 tank runs a little cooler.
