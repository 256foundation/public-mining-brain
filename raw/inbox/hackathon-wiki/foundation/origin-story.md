## Overview

The @256FOUNDATION account on X began as the voice of the POD256 podcast. Its 2023 posts are episode threads. In September 2023 the hosts announced a hashrate fundraiser they called a telehash. On 2024-02-02 they introduced a non-profit initiative for home and open source mining and asked listeners to help name it. By 2024-03-01 the posts use the name 256 Foundation. Later in 2024 the account announced its first named supporters and its first grant project. This article follows that path using only what the account posted. The archive is a keyword-search collection with known gaps, so missing steps do not mean nothing happened. See [X Archive of @256FOUNDATION](x-archive.md).

## 2023: a podcast account

The archive file for 2023 describes this period as the POD256 era, before the 501(c)(3) announcement. Every captured post belongs to an episode thread. The hosts named in the posts are @bitkite and @econoalchemist. Each thread ends by asking people to tag @pod256 to be featured on the show.

The guests in the second half of 2023 already point toward open and home mining:

| Date | Episode | Guest and subject |
|---|---|---|
| 2023-09-09 | 028 | Zack Bomsta (@zbomstaz), founder of @PivotalPlebTech, on the Njord and Loki boards and plans to "hyper-decentralize" mining |
| 2023-10-07 | 030 | @skot9000, billed as instigator of Bitaxe, which the post calls a fully open source ASIC bitcoin miner |
| 2023-10-30 | 031 | @BitMaker_, creator of the NerdMiner, described as an educational mining tool |
| 2023-12-18 | 032 | @Diverter_NoKYC and @SamouraiWallet on Ocean Mining and coinjoining |

For the wider episode record see [the POD256 episode guide for the early years](../pod256/episode-guide-2022-2023.md).

## The telehash idea

On 2023-09-23 the account posted episode 029, titled "Special Announcement: 24-Hour Telehash". The post says the hosts announce and discuss their plans for the first 24-hour "telehash". It describes it as a fundraiser to help communities across the world mine bitcoin. It was to be held at @bitcoinpark_ in January 2024.

> **Status: Disputed**
> The 2023-09-23 post plans a 24-hour event in January 2024 to help communities mine bitcoin. [Telehash](telehash.md) describes the first Telehash as a shorter livestream that raised funds for the foundation itself. The archive has no post showing a telehash held in January 2024, and no post saying the plan changed. The first posts about the block found at a Telehash are from March 2025 (see below).

## Early 2024: the nonprofit is introduced

- **2024-01-25.** The account posted an image with one word of text: "Soon". The same day it posted episode 033, a recap of the 2024 Nashville Energy & Mining Summit at Bitcoin Park.
- **2024-02-02.** Episode 034 is titled "Home & Open Source Mining Initiative". The post says the hosts "introduce a non-profit initiative to support home & open source bitcoin mining innovation, & need your help with a name." The archive file calls this the 256 Foundation origin post on X.
- **2024-03-01.** The post for episode 038 says the hosts "talk 256 Foundation progress". This is the earliest captured post whose text uses the name.
- **2024-04-19.** The post for episode 044 lists "256 Foundation updates" among its topics.

The archive does not capture a post that announces the chosen name. Coverage is missing for 3 to 9 Feb 2024, and episode 036 did not return in the search. The show notes side of this is in [POD256 Episode Guide: 2024](../pod256/episode-guide-2024.md).

## 2024: from show to organization

Through the first half of 2024 the account still posted mostly episode threads and Home Miner of the Week picks. In March 2024 it also ran weekly shoutouts for people supporting @POD256 with hashrate. See [Supporters and Sponsors Announced on X](supporters-and-sponsors.md).

The posts change in the second half of the year:

- **2024-08-15.** Episode 51 is titled "Mining Mondays and Making Bitcoin Mining Free and Open". The post says the show is back to weekly and the studio at Bitcoin Park got a major upgrade.
- **2024-09-19.** Episode 56 carries what the post calls a big announcement: @HRF throws support behind The 256 Foundation's mission to make Bitcoin mining free and open.
- **2024-09-27.** A follow-up post calls @HRF a new Tier II supporter.
- **2024-10-31.** POD256 moves from a weekly to a monthly cadence, recorded in front of a live audience during the Mining Monday Meetups at Bitcoin Park.
- **2024-11-11.** Episode 57 announces a new supporter, @heatbit_com, and the first 256 Foundation grant project, [Ember One](../hardware/ember-one.md).

## The block and the first grant cohort

The archive has no posts from December 2024 or January 2025, so the first Telehash itself is not captured. What follows it is:

- **2025-02-10.** The post for episode 60 lists "Fundraiser recap, upcoming grants, and more."
- **2025-03-22.** The account shared a clip of @harry_sudock describing "the epic moment we solo mined block 881423".
- **2025-03-25.** In a reply whose question is not captured, the account said it was running a @FutureBit Apollo as its node and stratum server, and that many miners from all over pointed their hashrate to it.
- **2025-04-05.** The account congratulated its first cohort of grant recipients and named four projects and their lead developers.

> **Status: Disputed**
> [Telehash](telehash.md) says the first Telehash found a block and launched the organization. The X posts show the initiative introduced on 2024-02-02, named supporters from 2024-09-19, and a first grant project announced on 2024-11-11. The archive does not give a date for the block. The two accounts fit if "launched" means the block paid for the first full grant cohort, but no source in this archive says so.

The grant cohort and what came after are in [X Timeline, 2025 to 2026](x-timeline-2025-2026.md).

## How the account describes itself now

The header of the 2026 archive file records the account as a 501(c)3 public charity with the bio "Building the open-source Bitcoin mining ecosystem." For the organization as it stands, see [256 Foundation: Mission and Organization](mission-and-organization.md) and [Nonprofit Record and Legal Status](nonprofit-record.md).
