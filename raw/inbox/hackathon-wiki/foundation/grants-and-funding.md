## Overview

The 256 Foundation both receives funding (from a Telehash block, the Human Rights Foundation, the MARA Foundation and others) and gives it out as grants to open-source mining work. It runs two grant programs, pays grantees monthly under written agreements, and never takes ownership of the resulting IP.

## The two grant programs

- **Core Projects Program.** Work the foundation scopes itself, for the four core projects: Ember One, Libre Board, Mujina and Hydrapool. Funded with priority under renewable terms.
- **General Grant Program.** Work the applicant scopes. Anything that advances the open-source Bitcoin mining ecosystem is eligible.

Eligible work includes open hardware designs (OSHWA-compliant), firmware and software (OSI-compliant open source), pool software, education and documentation, and supporting tools and libraries. Closed-source projects, unrelated projects, undifferentiated duplicates, and projects that restrict reuse are not eligible.

## How a grant runs

1. **Application.** A scoped proposal covering the gap, the project, the person, the license, milestones and reporting.
2. **Review.** The board reviews applications as they arrive against written criteria.
3. **Scope and agreement.** The board approves every grant. A written grant agreement keeps the work open source, and IP never transfers to the foundation.
4. **Build in public.** Work is paid monthly and developed in public under the project's license.

## Grants the foundation has made

| Project | Program | Term |
|---|---|---|
| [Ember One](../hardware/ember-one.md) | Core Projects Program | Six months, starting November 2024. The foundation's first grant. |
| [Libre Board](../hardware/libre-board.md) | Core Projects Program | Four months, September 2026 to December 2026. Reactivates a paused 2026 term. |

The Libre Board case shows the funding constraint plainly. Its 2026 term was set in April, was contingent on funding, and paused when funding ran short.

## Funding the foundation has received

- **First Telehash.** A block found during the first [Telehash](telehash.md) raised the initial funds that seeded the core projects.
- **Human Rights Foundation.** On August 25, 2026, HRF awarded a new grant through its Bitcoin Development Fund, continuing earlier support. The foundation said it would help polish and validate Libre Board, whose funding had been paused for months.
- **MARA Foundation.** On April 29, 2026, at the Bitcoin 2026 Conference in Las Vegas, the 256 Foundation won the MARA Foundation's inaugural $100,000 community contribution with 1,947 votes and 39% of the total, out of 4,971 votes cast. The other candidates were Libreria de Satoshi and SateNet. The money funds all four core projects.

## What the foundation says is scarce

In the HRF announcement the foundation says the constraint is not interest. It is grant-funded maintainer time to review community contributions, ship them, and validate them.
