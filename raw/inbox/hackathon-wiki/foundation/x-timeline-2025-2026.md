## Overview

This is a dated record of what the @256FOUNDATION account posted from 2025-02-10 to 2026-10-05. In these two years the account stops being only a podcast feed. It announces grant recipients and lead developers, hardware and software releases, Telehash events, supporters, and the Red Team program. The record comes from a keyword-search archive, not an official export, and it has gaps. See [X Archive of @256FOUNDATION](x-archive.md). Quoted posts from other accounts are those accounts' own words. Routine "POD256 is live" posts are left out unless they carry news.

### The grant cohort thread of 2025-04-05

The thread describes four projects and one pause. The opening post of the thread did not come back in the search; the replies did.

| Project | How the thread describes it | Lead developer | Cycle as posted |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ember One](../hardware/ember-one.md) | A standardized ~100W hashboard, eventually in several versions with ASICs from a number of manufacturers | @skot9000, with a background in EE and instigator of the Bitaxe | The second grant cycle starts in May and concludes in October |
| [Hydra Pool](../hydrapool/hydrapool.md) | A one-click deployable pool package that will eventually support Stratum v2 and multiple payout models | @jungly, with a PhD in distributed systems, also developing P2Pool v2, and a former Braidpool dev | Starts now, ends in September |
| [Libre Board](../hardware/libre-board.md) | The control board for the Ember One mining system, with standardized connections for compute modules, temperature sensors, relays and more | @Schnitzel | Starts now, ends in September |
| [Mujina](../mujina/mujina-firmware.md) | Starts as the mining application for the Ember One and will eventually be a full Linux image with Stratum V2 support | @ryankuester, with 20yrs of embedded Linux systems design and a background in EE | Starts now, ends in December |

A fifth project, Block Watcher, was paused. The thread gives the reason: the other 4 projects are more central to the mission, and at the foundation's early stage and support level it made more sense to put the capital where it counts most.

The thread says the four projects are the critical pieces of a complete, fully open-source mining system that ends dependence on conglomerates like Bitmain.

On 2025-04-30 the Ember One release post credits @skot9000 with designing the board from scratch over the last six months. This matches the six-month first term in [Grants and Funding](grants-and-funding.md).
