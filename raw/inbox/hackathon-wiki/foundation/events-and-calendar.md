## Overview

The 256 Foundation keeps one public calendar on its forum. Telehash dates, live podcast recordings and developer calls are all posted there, and the foundation calls it the best single place to follow upcoming events. Most entries are recurring Mujina developer calls. The team also appears at outside conferences, including two panels at Bitcoin Las Vegas 2026.

## The forum calendar

The calendar lives at the forum's upcoming-events page. As collected, it held three kinds of event.

## Conference appearances

- **Bitcoin Las Vegas 2026.** The team was on two 256 Foundation panels. Tyler posted the two YouTube recordings on the forum on 2026-05-14.
- **MARA Foundation vote.** On 2026-04-27 Tyler asked the forum to vote over the next two days in a MARA Foundation contest giving $100k to one organization helping bitcoin. On 2026-05-02 he posted that the foundation had won. The award is covered in [Grants and Funding](grants-and-funding.md).
- **Presidio Bitcoin, San Francisco.** The foundation's first private, in-person fundraiser. See [Presidio Bitcoin Fundraiser and the Case for Commoditizing Mining](presidio-bitcoin-fundraiser.md).
- **Bitcoin++ in Nairobi.** The Presidio post mentions Ryan teaching the stack in person there.
- **Heatpunk Summit.** The annual Denver gathering of the Hashrate Heatpunks community. See [Hashrate Heatpunks](hashrate-heatpunks.md).
