## Overview

The 256 Foundation is a 501(c)(3) public charity registered in Nashville, Tennessee. Its IRS record, as shown by ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer, gives its EIN, ruling date and a first set of financial figures. No filings were listed yet when the record was collected. The foundation's FAQ fills in how that status affects donors and grantees.

## What the foundation says about its status

The FAQ gives the same tax ID, written as 99-1662333, and calls the foundation a 501(c)(3) public charity. It says the foundation was founded in February 2024.

Three practical consequences follow in the FAQ:

- **Donors.** Donations are tax-deductible as allowed by law. No products or services are exchanged for them.
- **Grantees.** Because the foundation must file taxes, it needs taxpayer information from grant recipients. A grantee who wants to stay pseudonymous can form a Wyoming LLC and share that entity's information.
- **Transparency.** Individual grant amounts are kept confidential for security reasons. Annual tax disclosures are available for public inspection, in line with IRS requirements.

The FAQ also states that no board member is compensated.
