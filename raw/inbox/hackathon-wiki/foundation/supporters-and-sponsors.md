## Overview

This article lists who backed the 256 Foundation and when, as the @256FOUNDATION account announced it on X. Support came in four forms: money from named organizations, hashrate pointed by individual miners, sponsorship of the newsletter, and donated chips. The named organizations are the Human Rights Foundation, Heatbit, OpenSats, Proto and the MARA Foundation. This list is only as complete as the archive behind it, which is a keyword search with gaps. A supporter missing here may simply fall in a gap. See [X Archive of @256FOUNDATION](x-archive.md). For the foundation's own account of its funding, see [Grants and Funding](grants-and-funding.md).

## Human Rights Foundation over time

HRF is the supporter the account returns to most often.

- **2024-09-19.** First announcement, on episode 56.
- **2024-09-27.** Named as a Tier II supporter.
- **2025-05-28.** "@HRF continues their support of the 256 Foundation with another grant!" The post quotes @femilonge, who writes that with HRF's support Ember One is decentralizing mining so that anyone can mine, even in repressive countries. Those are his words.
- **2026-08-25.** The account thanks HRF for continued support. It quotes @gladstein, who says HRF's Bitcoin Development Fund is granting more than 500 million sats to 16 freedom tech projects worldwide. The quoted post does not say how much goes to the 256 Foundation.
- **2026-08-31.** The account says the grant will help polish the latest revisions and validate hardware, while continuing Mujina firmware development and ecosystem work.

## Sponsor tiers

The only tier named in the captured posts is Tier II, used once, for HRF on 2024-09-27. No captured post explains the tiers or names a Tier I supporter. The POD256 show notes carry a fuller supporter list; see [POD256 Episode Guide: 2024](../pod256/episode-guide-2024.md).

## The MARA Foundation vote

On 2026-04-29 the account asked followers for a vote, describing the mission as building the open-source mining stack and funding developers. Later that day it thanked the MARA Foundation and everyone who voted. It called the contest an awesome race alongside @libdesatoshi and @satenetorg and asked followers to keep supporting those projects too. The vote count behind the result is in [Grants and Funding](grants-and-funding.md).

## Hashrate supporters

People have backed the show and the foundation with hashrate since before the foundation had a name.

**POD256 hashers, March 2024.** On 2024-03-12, 2024-03-19 and 2024-03-26 the account posted video shoutouts to people supporting @POD256 with hashrate over the previous week. The instructions were to pick any Lincoin URL, use "pod256.YourAlias" as the worker name, and listen to the next episode to hear the alias read out. The readers were @penny_ether, @econoalchemist and @zbomstaz.

**The block.** In a 2025-03-25 reply, posted days after a clip about solo mining block 881423, the account said many miners from all over pointed their hashrate to it. The question it was answering is not captured, and the archive does not name the miners.

**Telehash #2.** On 2025-04-30 the account opened Hydra Pool for public testing and asked for hashrate ahead of the Cinco De Mayo event. On 2025-05-21 it asked supporters to redirect their miners, with 21 workers still pointing ~1 Ph/s.

**Telehash #4.** On 2026-05-22 the account thanked its top ten contributors:

1. @ElektronEnergy
2. @NiceHashMining
3. @Schnitzel
4. @FutureBit
5. @megawatthq
6. @tronsington
7. @RiglyMining
8. A contributor identified only by a Nostr profile link
9. @Exergy_LLC
10. @compass_mining

The day before, it thanked all contributors to what it called its 4th fundraiser. After the event it said people can continue supporting the foundation with hash and will be read out on POD256. More in [Telehash 4](telehash-4.md).

## Donated chips

- **Intel BZM2.** On 2025-04-24 the account said it had 256k BZM2 chips ready to send to manufacturers and developers for free, and could get more. On 2025-10-31 it named Reckless Systems as one of the donation recipients. The captured posts do not name the donor. Other sources do; see [Foundation Progress Timeline, 2025](../newsletter/foundation-progress-2025.md) and [Intel BZM2 Designs: BIRDS and Bonanza](../ecosystem/intel-bzm2-designs.md).
- **Auradine Treasure B1.** On 2025-11-19 the account said an anonymous donor sent a brand new reel of @Auradine_Inc Treasure B1 ASICs. It offered them free in cut tape quantities of 25, with the recipient paying postage and no documentation. The post lists them as "14J/Th 3nm (we think)". It adds that the initiative is not supported by Auradine.

## Other backing mentioned

- **Bitcoin Park.** Not announced as a sponsor, but present throughout. The podcast studio is there, and the Mining Monday Meetups where episodes were recorded took place there.
- **Hardware for the Red Team.** On 2026-08-17 the account asked for support with hardware, since auditing the mining stack requires hardware to test firmware. See [Red Team Program](red-team-program.md).
- **General appeals.** On 2026-04-27 and 2026-09-14 the account asked for contributors and donors. The ways to give are in [Donate and Get Involved](donate-and-get-involved.md).

## What the archive cannot show

- Amounts. Apart from the MARA Foundation figure, no captured post states how much a supporter gave.
- Supporters announced in the gaps. December 2024 and January 2025 are missing, as are other stretches.
- Anything the account announced only on the podcast, the newsletter or the website.
