## Overview

This is a dated record of what the @256FOUNDATION account posted from 2023-07-22 to 2024-11-11. In this period the account is mostly the POD256 podcast feed: episode threads, Home Miner of the Week picks, and listener shoutouts. The foundation appears partway through, with the nonprofit initiative on 2024-02-02, HRF support on 2024-09-19 and the first grant project on 2024-11-11. The record comes from a keyword-search archive, not an official export. It has gaps, listed in [X Archive of @256FOUNDATION](x-archive.md). Quoted posts from other accounts are those accounts' own words.

## How the posts are shaped

A typical episode in this period is a thread of three or four posts:

1. The episode number, title, guests and a one-line summary.
2. Links to listen on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Breez and Fountain.
3. A Home Miner of the Week pick, which quotes someone else's post. It ends by asking people to tag @pod256 (later @POD256) to be featured.

From August 2024 the threads get shorter and often carry only a title and a link.
