## Overview

POD256 is a Bitcoin podcast about open-source Bitcoin mining, energy and freedom tech. Its home page says it is recorded weekly at Bitcoin Park in Nashville, TN and co-hosted by @econoalchemist, @skot9000 and @tylerkstevens. The first episode was published on 2022-12-03 and the show notes in this wiki run to episode 127, published on 2026-09-28. The show began as a weekly mining-industry news program and turned into the public voice of the [256 Foundation](../foundation/mission-and-organization.md), whose founders host it.

The home page lists three co-hosts and does not list Rod. The show notes do not say when or why that changed. Episode 104 describes Skot and Tyler as freshly minted 256 Foundation board members.

## How the format changed

1. **Guest interviews (episodes 001 to 003).** One guest, a short topic list and resource links.
2. **Segment show (episodes 004 to 046).** State of the Network, News, Home Miner of the Week and a guest slot called No Holds Barred. The notes give the recording date, block height and a halving countdown, and link the same data sources each week: a difficulty estimator, mempool.space, Luxor's Hashrate Index and Braiins Insights.
3. **Prose summaries (from episode 047).** The notes become written summaries. Guests shift toward pool and hardware builders.
4. **Keywords and chapters (episodes 061 to 080).** Notes list keywords, takeaways and timestamped chapters.
5. **Project log (from episode 081).** Dense paragraphs in the hosts' voice, mostly about the open-source mining stack, heat reuse and freedom tech.

Episode 100 says it marks three years of POD256 and looks back to the early "Hash Cast" days.

Two longer breaks are noted: episode 027 mentions a two week hiatus and episode 125 opens after a short hiatus. The notes for episode 126 say Eco called it #125 on air by mistake.

## Where it is recorded

The home page says Bitcoin Park in Nashville. Episode 051 describes studio upgrades courtesy of Bitcoin Park, and episode 057 was recorded in front of a live audience there on November 4, 2024. Episode 056 describes the idea of recording at the monthly Monday mining meetup.

Not every episode comes from Nashville. Episodes 084 and 085 were recorded at the Proto Mining Rig launch event, the second night in Dalton, Georgia. Episode 090 took live updates from TabConf. Episode 097 has Tyler and eco catching up on winter in Colorado. Episode 106 was recorded from the Hashtub in Denver and episode 108 was a live episode from Denver.

In 2026 live recordings were announced on the 256 Foundation forum, for example episode 114 at 2100 UTC on a Wednesday. A forum thread titled Past Recordings points back to the podcast website.

## How listeners support the show

- **Pointing hashrate.** From episode 006 the notes ask listeners to pick a Lincoin pool URL and use a `pod256` worker name. Each week the hosts read out the worker names that pointed hash. Episode 043 also thanks hashers on Public Pool.
- **Donations.** Episode 008 says POD256 enabled a PayNym donation method.
- **Supporters.** From episode 052 the notes list Tier II Supporters, starting with Foundry USA.
- **Later.** Shout-outs move to hashers on the 256 Foundation's own pool. Episode 094 thanks hashers across Lincoin, Solo CK, Public Pool and Ocean. Episode 103 asks listeners to point hashrate to the foundation's donation-only Hydra Pool instance, and episode 112 points to a leaderboard at dash.256f.org.

## The show and the 256 Foundation

The foundation's story can be followed through the notes.

- Episode 029 lays out the telehash idea as a 24-hour fundraising event.
- Episode 034 is titled for a Home & Open Source Mining Initiative.
- Episode 035 lists the working name of an open source mining non-profit: The 256 Foundation.
- Episode 038 links a 256 Foundation Telegram group.
- Episode 051 states the mission: to make Bitcoin mining free and open.
- Episode 060 reports that the Telehash hit a block, after what the notes call two years of planning.
- Episode 100 reviews the grants and the four projects they fund.

From then on most episodes are progress reports. See [Open-Source Mining Stack Coverage on POD256](open-source-mining-stack-coverage.md).
