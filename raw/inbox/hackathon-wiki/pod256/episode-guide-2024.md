## Overview

This guide covers POD256 episodes 033 to 058, published from 2024-01-25 to 2024-12-26. The year splits in two. Up to episode 046 the show kept its segment format and ran a halving countdown that reached one day in episode 044. From episode 047 the notes become prose summaries, the guests shift from mining executives to pool and hardware builders, and the hosts start talking about the 256 Foundation and its mission to make Bitcoin mining free and open.

## Pool centralization in episode 044

The news list in episode 044 has skot saying “REJECT MINING CENTRALIZATION” and a claim from @mononautical, with a supporting thread from @0xB10C:

> “A single custodian now controls the coinbase addresses of at least 9 pools, representing 47% of total hashrate.”

The pool-focused guests that follow (Luke Dashjr, Wilson Mining, boerst, Bob McElrath) all come after this item.

## The 256 Foundation takes shape

- Episode 034 is titled for a Home & Open Source Mining Initiative.
- Episode 035 lists as news: "Open source mining non-profit working name: The 256 Foundation".
- Episode 038 links a 256 Foundation Telegram group.
- Episode 051 gives the mission as making Bitcoin mining free and open, and says the foundation accepts donations from all sources while staying independent.
- Episode 056 reports significant support from the Human Rights Foundation.
- Episode 057 describes Ember One as a project to create a fully open-source, 100-watt hash board.

For the foundation itself see [256 Foundation: Mission and Organization](../foundation/mission-and-organization.md).

## Supporters named in the notes

From episode 052 the notes carry a "Tier II Supporters" list. It starts with Foundry USA. HRF is added in episode 056 and Heatbit in episode 057.
