## Overview

This guide covers POD256 episodes 101 to 127, published from 2026-01-14 to 2026-09-28, plus three forum threads that announced live recordings. By now the show is hosted by eco, Skot and Tyler and is mostly about the open-source mining stack: Mujina running on more and more hardware, Hydra Pool and its dashboard, Telehash #3 and #4, and home heating with miners. AI tooling becomes a regular topic from episode 104 onward. Late in the run the show also covers the Coldcard exploit and the BIP 110 controversy.
