## Overview

This guide covers POD256 episodes 001 to 032, published from 2022-12-03 to 2023-12-18. In this first era the show was a weekly mining-industry news program. Most episodes followed a fixed run of segments: State of the Network, News, Home Miner of the Week, and a guest interview called No Holds Barred. Guests came mostly from mining companies, with a growing number of home miners and open-hardware builders toward the end of 2023. Episode 029 is the first place the show notes describe the Telehash idea.

## How the show notes are laid out

- Episodes 001 to 003 list one guest, a short bullet list of topics, and resource links.
- From episode 004 the notes say that Econoalchemist and Rod cover the week's items, with no guest required.
- Episode 005 adds the date, the block height, and the Home Miner of the Week segment.
- Episode 006 adds a halving countdown and the "Stream Sats, Point Hash" block, which asks listeners to point miners at Lincoin for the show.
- Episode 029 is the first written as a prose summary. Episodes 030 to 032 return to the segment layout.

Block heights and halving countdowns for each episode are collected in [State of the Network Snapshots](state-of-the-network-snapshots.md).

## A disputed news item: who was buying ASICs

Episode 015 listed as news that more ASICs were being sold in Russia than in the USA, and named Marty Bent as the source. Episode 016 repeated the item and then added a counter-claim from Luxor.

> **Status: Disputed**
> Episode 015 and episode 016 report, citing Marty Bent, that more ASICs were being sold in Russia than in the USA. Episode 016 also reports that the Luxor team contradicts this in its report, which suggests North and South American miners bought the majority of ASICs, and notes that Russia does not appear on Luxor's list. The show notes do not settle which claim is right.

## Notable firsts

- Episode 006 starts hash pointing: listeners pick a Lincoin URL and use a `pod256` worker name.
- Episode 018 is the first appearance of Schnitzel in the notes, as Home Miner of the Week for an immersion system in a cooler. He returns as a guest in episodes 022 and 024.
- Episode 030 is skot's first appearance. He later becomes a co-host.
- Episode 032 is the show's first Samourai Wallet episode. See [Samourai Wallet and Developer Prosecutions on POD256](samourai-and-developer-prosecutions.md).
