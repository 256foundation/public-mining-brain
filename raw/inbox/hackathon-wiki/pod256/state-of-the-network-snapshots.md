## Overview

Between episode 005 and episode 046, POD256 show notes opened with a snapshot of the Bitcoin network: the block height on recording day and, from episode 006, a countdown to the halving. From episode 031 the notes also wrote out difficulty, hashrate and hashvalue in words. Read in order, they give a week-by-week record of the network in the run-up to the 2024 halving, as the hosts saw it. This article collects those figures. All values are copied as written in the show notes, including their formatting.

## Where the numbers came from

The segment linked the same sources each week:

- A difficulty estimator: Bitrawr at first, then Newhedge from episode 030.
- mempool.space for hashrate and difficulty.
- Hashrate Index from Luxor for ASIC prices.
- Braiins Insights for hashvalue.

The halving countdown is credited to bitcoinblockhalf.com in episode 021.

> **Status: Disputed**
> The difficulty figures around epoch 411 do not agree. Episode 035 (2024-02-10) gives 75.5T. Episode 036 (2024-02-16) gives 70.3T, the same value printed in episodes 033 and 034, while still expecting an increase at the end of epoch 411. Episode 037 then gives 81.7 Trillion. The episode 036 figure may be a leftover from earlier notes, but the show notes do not correct it.

## Other figures in the notes

- **Mempool.** Episode 034 notes approximately 250k pending transactions, with transactions paying less than 24 sats/v-byte being purged.
- **Leap day.** Episode 037 predicted, and episode 038 reported, that the epoch 412 adjustment would land on February 29th, which the notes call the only difficulty adjustment on a leap-year day in Bitcoin's history.
- **ASIC prices.** From episode 039 to episode 043 the notes say machines under 25J/Th were selling for roughly $13.73/Th, flat since October. Episodes 044 and 046 give $12.21/Th.
- **Hashprice.** Episode 043 gives roughly $103/Ph/day and episode 044 gives $102/Ph/Day.

## After the halving

Episode 046 (2024-05-24) is the last full snapshot. It gives block height 844622, with a note left in for Rod or Eco to update it. Difficulty is 83.1 Trillion with an increase of roughly 1% expected at the end of epoch 418. Hashrate is 591Eh, down from an all-time high of 639Eh on April 27th. Hashvalue is 80k sats/Ph/day and hashprice is $56/Ph/Day. The halving countdown has reset: the next one is due in 1,407 days.

Compared with episode 044, hashvalue went from 160k sats/PH/day to 80k sats/Ph/day and hashprice from $102/Ph/Day to $56/Ph/Day.

From episode 047 the show notes turn to prose and stop printing these figures. Later episodes mention network updates only in passing.
