## Overview

This guide covers POD256 episodes 060 to 100, published from 2025-02-06 to 2025-12-31. The year opens with the hosts explaining how the first Telehash found a block, and it closes with a 100th episode that reviews the 256 Foundation's grants. In between, the show becomes the running public log of the foundation's four projects (Ember One, Libre Board, Mujina and Hydra Pool) and follows the Samourai Wallet case from plea to prison sentence. Tyler joins the show in episode 081. The in-scope sources contain no show notes for an episode 059.

## How the show notes are laid out

- Episodes 061 to 080 use a Keywords, Summary, Takeaways and Chapters layout. Several of these read like machine transcriptions and misspell project names (see the note at the end).
- From episode 081 the notes are one or two dense paragraphs written in the hosts' voice.

## Milestones in this run

- **Hosts.** Episode 081 welcomes Tyler. Episode 082 names the hosts as Skot, eco and Tyler. Rod still appears: episode 088 has him thanking the ImagineIF team and presenters.
- **Live episodes.** Episodes 084 and 085 were recorded at the Proto launch, 087 at Bitcoin Park, and 090 took live updates from TabConf.
- **Samourai.** The case runs through episodes 070, 072, 080, 093, 094, 096, 097, 098, 099 and 100. See [Samourai Wallet and Developer Prosecutions on POD256](samourai-and-developer-prosecutions.md).
- **Episode 100.** The notes say the foundation allocated over $400k in grants across Ember One, Libre Board, Mujina and Hydra Pool, and recall the Telehash night when a solo pool struck block 881423.

## A note on spellings

The transcribed notes spell some names several ways. Mujina appears as "Mugenia" in episode 067. Hydra Pool appears as "Hydro Pool", "HydroPool", "Hydrapool" and "HydraPool". Bitaxe appears as "Bidax", "BitAx" and "BitAxe". Libre Board appears as "LibreBoard" and "LibriBoard". This guide uses one spelling for each.
