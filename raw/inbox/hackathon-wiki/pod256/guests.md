## Overview

POD256 has hosted a wide mix of guests. In 2022 and 2023 most came from mining companies, analysts and energy people. From late 2023 the guest list tilts toward people who build open hardware, pools and firmware, and by 2025 many guests are developers funded by or working with the 256 Foundation. This article groups the guests named in the show notes by what they represent, with the episodes they appear in. Titles and affiliations are given as the show notes stated them at the time.
