## Overview

The @Public_Pool_BTC account posts about the Public Pool solo mining pool and about open hardware its operator builds. This article gathers its archived posts from 2025 and 2026: block finds, near misses, Stratum V2 support, and claims about vendors and licenses. The posts come from a keyword-search sample. For what Public Pool is, see [Public Pool](public-pool.md).

## Near misses and difficulty

- **2025-11-07.** A 20T difficulty share came in from a ++ miner on hosted public-pool.
- **2026-02-03.** Public Pool said it was about to get 15% easier to solo mine a block, with a target difficulty of about 120T.
- **2026-04-04.** After the block the day before, another NerdOCTAXE submitted a 59T share 90 minutes later. Public Pool put that at 43% of the network difficulty.

## Stratum V2 and app store updates

On 2026-06-05 Public Pool made two announcements:

1. With Stratum V2 support released in Bitaxe/ESP-Miner (v2.14.0), the hosted pool at web.public-pool.io now supports Stratum V2. Miners turn on SV2 in advanced pool options and enter the pool's public key. The post thanked warioishere for the implementation details.
2. Several months of improvements were merged into the main sqlite branch and pushed to the Umbrel and Start9 app stores, pending their approval. Upgrading was recommended for people running the pool at home.

See [AxeOS / ESP-Miner Firmware](axeos-esp-miner-firmware.md).

## Looking back at open hardware

On 2025-08-17 Public Pool reflected on progress since an earlier post. It said the Bitcoin network hashrate had increased 2X while the hashrate of open-source miners had surged 10X, from the 201 Ultra to the Nerdqaxe++. It added that production capacity is far higher, prices are lower, and availability is global. It pointed to eight consecutive blocks from Foundry that day as the reason the work matters.

On 2026-05-14 it argued that the home mining market was stagnant before Bitaxe, with the only choices being USB sticks or full sized machines.

## Claims about vendors and licenses

These are Public Pool's statements, reported as made. The archive holds no reply from the parties.

- **2026-05-06.** Addressing a party the archived text does not name, Public Pool said it was glad they liked its rev 6.1 design, accused them of copyright infringement, and asked them to release their changes, including for other open source derived products.
- **2026-07-28.** Public Pool said ASIC series architecture had been done with the original Bitaxe Hex years earlier. In the same post it claimed that "hammer" took esp-miner and made it closed source for its products.

Related claims by Skot are in [Skot on Bitaxe and Copyleft](skot-on-bitaxe-and-copyleft.md).
