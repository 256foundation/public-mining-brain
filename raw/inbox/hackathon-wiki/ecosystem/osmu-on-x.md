## Overview

Open Source Miners United runs the X account @osmu_global. The account joined 2024-03-05 and links to osmu.xyz and the OSMU Discord. It posts short notes: firmware releases, block finds by open miners, thanks to contributors, and sharp words about closed-source copies of Bitaxe. The archive says Discord is the primary OSMU surface and X is secondary. For what OSMU is, see [Bitaxe and Open Source Miners United](bitaxe-and-osmu.md).

- **2025-05-30.** Quoted WantClue's video for Bitaxe update Version 2.8.0.

## The license stance

The account repeats one message: open source or nothing. It also makes claims about other sellers. These are OSMU's statements and name no company in the archived text.

- **2024-09-02.** A short verse included the line "GPL violated, yet pioneers".
- **2024-11-25.** Said that more home miners now exist, but OSMU and Bitaxe are so far the only truly open source project.
- **2025-06-10.** "Don’t get fooled. OSMU is copyleft not copyright."
- **2025-06-25.** Reminded buyers that the original Bitaxe is an open-source project from Skot and the OSMU team, and warned about proprietary devices using the Bitaxe name.
- **2025-08-09 and 2025-10-05.** Criticized unnamed sellers who say they dislike OSMU and still sell products built from its work.
- **2025-08-12.** Said OSMU project licences are copyleft, that funded projects' repository owners received their compensation, and that each manufacturer decides alone whether to donate.
- **2026-05-14.** Said companies creating a closed source copy of Bitaxe shows the project is doing the right thing.

Skot's own license statements are in [Skot on Bitaxe and Copyleft](skot-on-bitaxe-and-copyleft.md).

## Tone

Many posts are a single line, such as "Open Source is the only option." (2026-06-28). On 2024-05-15 the account quoted @Pmaxsd saying the Axe miners were mainly meant to run as lottery miners that support decentralization. On 2024-04-25 it asked why more people do not post about running a local pool at home with Umbrel and Public Pool, which it said takes 30sec to set up. On 2024-08-26 it warned about impersonation and suspicious links.
