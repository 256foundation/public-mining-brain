# Canaan Mujina Port

> Sources: Aadhi Maalaven (@unknown_aadhi) and Tyler Stevens (@tylerkstevens) on X, 2026-08-12 to 2026-10-03; Michael Schmid (@Schnitzel) on X, in the notable open-source mining archive, 2026-08-12; all collected 2026-10-07
> Raw: [X archive: notable Canaan / Mujina port posts](../../raw/inbox/2026-10-07-x-notable-canaan-mujina-port.md); [X archive: notable open-source mining posts](../../raw/inbox/2026-10-07-x-notable-open-source-mining.md)
> Updated: 2026-10-07

## Overview

In August 2026 a community developer, Aadhi Maalaven (@unknown_aadhi), posted that Mujina was running on a Canaan Nano 3S. Aadhi said they reverse engineered the miner, built the port, and made it open source. The posts below are Aadhi's own account of the work. The archive holds no repository link, test results or independent confirmation. For Mujina itself, see [Mujina Firmware](../mujina/mujina-firmware.md).

## What is and is not known

- The Nano 3S port is reported as running, by its author.
- The Mini 3 work is reported as started, not finished.
- The Red Team's own Mini 3 plans are stated as intent on 2026-08-12. The archive has no later update on them. See [Red Team Program](../foundation/red-team-program.md).
- The archive says the full reply graph under the original post is missing.
