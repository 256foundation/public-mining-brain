## Overview

Hashrate Heatpunks talks in three public places: a Discourse forum, a Telegram group, and an X account. The forum is where the content lives. The X account mostly relays forum threads. This article describes each channel and its size as of 2026-10-07. For the community's mission and the summit, see [Hashrate Heatpunks](../foundation/hashrate-heatpunks.md).

### The summit announcement

Tyler Stevens (@tylerkstevens) announced the third Heatpunk Summit on 2026-07-22: Feb 26-27 in Denver, CO. The post gave each year a theme. Year 1 was the first gathering, year 2 formalized the movement, and year 3 is "The open-source stack is here". It pointed to a waitlist at heatpunks.org/summit. The post had 52 likes at collection. The archive notes that the summit story lives on Tyler's account more than on @heatpunks.

## The Telegram group

The Telegram group @heatpunks is a supergroup titled Hashrate Heatpunks. Its description calls it the official group chat, a community of builders working on the emerging hashrate heating industry. The stated mission is to marry the bitcoin mining and heating sectors to bring hashrate back home. It links to heatpunks.org for resources and the forum.

- Members on 2026-10-07: 175, with 21 online at that moment.
- Members in an earlier read on 2026-09-05: 171.

No messages were collected. The reading bot joined after the group existed and found nothing in its queue, and the public web preview shows no history for groups. The source warns that an empty queue does not mean the group is quiet.

## How the channels fit together

- The forum holds long-form builds, questions and announcements.
- X repeats forum headlines to a wider audience.
- Telegram is the live chat. Its content is not in this wiki.
