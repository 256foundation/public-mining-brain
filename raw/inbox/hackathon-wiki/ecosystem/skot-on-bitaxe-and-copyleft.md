# Skot on Bitaxe and Copyleft

> Sources: skot (@skot9000) on X, 2026-04-21 to 2026-09-03, collected 2026-10-07; mixed accounts on X (notable open-source mining posts), 2025-06-05 to 2026-10-06, collected 2026-10-07

## Overview

Skot (@skot9000) started the Bitaxe project and sits on the 256 Foundation board. This article collects what Skot posted on X about Bitaxe, its copyleft license, and vendors Skot says broke that license. The posts come from a keyword-search archive, not a full timeline. Statements about named vendors below are Skot's own claims. The archive holds no reply from those vendors, and this wiki has not checked the claims.

## The copyleft position

Skot stated the license terms twice in mid-2026.

- On 2026-06-18 Skot wrote that Bitaxe is open source hardware and software/firmware under a copyleft license. Anyone may use, audit, modify, share and distribute it. The condition is that modified versions cannot be distributed without the complete source under the same license. The post called doing otherwise illegal copyright infringement, and closed with "Your users deserve the same freedom you have."
- On 2026-07-01 Skot put it more bluntly: if you sell a modified version, publishing the source is "the deal". The post tied this to miners knowing what they run.

On 2026-09-20 Skot added that open source Bitcoin miners are not in the business of extracting cash from unsuspecting buyers.

## Claims about vendors and the GPL

These are Skot's statements, reported as made. Each post carried an image that the archive links but does not describe, so the evidence Skot pointed to is not in the source.

- **HammerMiner, 2026-08-27.** Skot posted that HammerMiner had admitted to using Bitaxe's GPL esp-miner code in closed source miner firmware.
- **MagicMiner, 2026-09-02.** Skot posted that MagicMiner violates the Bitaxe esp-miner GPL, and said the company had publicly confirmed it.

Other accounts made related claims. See [Public Pool on X](public-pool-on-x.md) and [Open Mining Voices on X](open-mining-voices-on-x.md).

## Praise for open source use

Not every vendor post is a complaint. On 2026-09-22 Skot welcomed Chinese hardware manufacturers deploying and supporting open source, with a wave to Bitmain's account. On 2026-09-03 he quoted a Vinteum blog post that said its authors used community reverse engineering documentation plus Mujina and Bitaxe to connect the protocol view of mining with real hardware. His comment was "THIS IS HOW WE WIN".

## The "Bitaxe in every home" post

On 2025-06-05 Skot answered the phrase "Bitaxe in every home". The post said that if it means a very large global group of miners running open source hardware, firmware and software, then yes, that fixes Bitcoin mining. It listed what that covers: a wide range of hashrate, decentralized home mining, stranded energy, heat reuse, and uses not yet imagined. It described those miners as largely immune to regulation, able to run through periods of low fiat profitability, and not dependent on a single large manufacturer's equipment. The post had 274 likes at collection.
