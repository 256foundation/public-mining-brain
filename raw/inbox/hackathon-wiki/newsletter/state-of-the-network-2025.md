## Overview

Six newsletter issues, May to October 2025, ended with a State of the Network section. Each reported the previous month's Bitcoin hashrate, difficulty, ASIC prices, hashvalue and the countdown to the next halving. Together they cover April through September 2025. Over that time the hashrate moving average went from about 826 Eh/s to about 1,060 Eh/s, with one sharp dip in June. The section was dropped in the November issue, which pointed readers to live sources instead. The figures below are the newsletter's readings of mempool.space, Luxor's Hashrate Index and Braiins Insights. They are snapshots, and a few do not line up from one issue to the next.

## Hashrate

Figures are in Eh/s on the mempool.space moving average. The first five issues used the 14-day average. The October issue switched to the 7-day average.

| Month | Start | End | Change in month | Year to date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| April | about 826 | about 841 | about 1.8% growth | not given |
| May | about 837 | about 917 | about 9.5% growth | about 17% |
| June | about 915 | 840 | roughly -8.1% | +6.8% |
| July | about 832 | 906 | roughly +8.8% | +15.2% |
| August | about 913 | about 966 | roughly +5.8% | +22.9% |
| September | about 969 | about 1,060 | roughly +9.4% | +34.8% |

- April peaked at about 907 Eh/s on April 16 before cooling off.
- The end of May was a new all-time high.
- The October issue also writes the September close as 1.06 Zh/s.
- The end figure for one month and the start figure for the next are different readings and do not match exactly.
- The November issue, summarizing a podcast from October 29, mentions network growth to 1.12 Zeta hash.

## Difficulty

| Month | Change in month | Year to date |
|---|---|---|
| April | 8.3% increase | about 8.5% up to Epoch #444 |
| May | 6.6% increase | about 15.6% up to Epoch #446 |
| June | about 7.8% decrease | about 6.5% up to Epoch #448 |
| July | about 8.4% increase | about 16.2% up to Epoch #450 |
| August | 1.6% increase | about 18.1% up to Epoch #452 |
| September | 9.7% increase | about 29.6% up to Epoch #455 |

- Epoch #444, on May 3, brought a -3.3% downward adjustment just after April ended.
- On June 28 difficulty dropped about 7.5% in one adjustment. The newsletter called it the biggest downward adjustment since the Chinese mining ban of summer 2021, when difficulty fell by almost 40% in a single adjustment. Suggested causes ranged from interrupted mining in Iran to large miners curtailing during a Texas heat wave.
- July's rise brought difficulty back to where it was before that drop.
- Difficulty only went up in August and September. It ended August at 129.7T and ended September at 142.3T.

The May and June issues add that a new-generation miner with 230+ Th/s cost roughly $4,000.

> **Status: Disputed**
> The October issue's look back at August does not match the September issue. The September issue gives August prices of $13.92/Th for the most efficient tier and $3.64/Th for the least efficient. The October issue says the same tiers were $13.96/Th and $3.92/Th last month. For the middle tier the September issue gives $7.56/Th, while the October issue says it was $8.57/Th at the beginning of the month.
