## Description

Yes, me again. I hope I'm not annoying anyone!  🫣

There are two things that I think could be improved in the scoreboard display:

**Difficulty**
As in the dashboard or swarm, the difficulty should always be displayed with two decimal places. It would also be visually better to display this right-aligned. 

**Date and time**
Here, I would recommend separating the time from the date with a tab stop and a slightly larger space. This would improve readability. Even though it is probably only needed very rarely, I would find it more appealing.
