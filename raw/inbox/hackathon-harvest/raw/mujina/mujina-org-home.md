## Own your firmware

You bought the hardware, but closed firmware means someone else controls it. Mujina is GPL-licensed Rust you can read, audit, and change without permission.

Why Mujina exists

Mining firmware you own: read every line, change what you like, pay no dev fee. One codebase for hardware from any vendor.

Mujina aims to be the Linux kernel of Bitcoin mining firmware: a single open source codebase that drives mining hardware from any vendor, maintained in the open by the people who use it. Today Mujina is a daemon you build and run yourself. The goal is Mujina OS: complete operating system images installed onto a miner's control board.

[Why Mujina](https://mujina.org/explanation/why-mujina) explains the thinking; [Your First Run](https://mujina.org/tutorial/first-run) shows it working on your computer in minutes, no mining hardware required.
