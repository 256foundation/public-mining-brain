# Bitcoin mining will be open-source,or Bitcoin remains permissioned

We're building the open-source 

Bitcoin mining stack.

Bitcoin mining does three jobs.

It issues new coins, settles transactions, and secures the record.

Today, that machinery is closed.

The machines this whole industry runs on are unauditable, unfixable, and permissioned.

## It takes the recipes.

A miner is four unique building blocks. We open-sourced all four.

[HardwareEmber OneOpen hashboard reference designView →](https://256foundation.org/projects#ember-one)

[HardwareLibre BoardOpen control board reference designView →](https://256foundation.org/projects#libre-board)

[FirmwareMujinaOpen mining firmware, the Linux OS of Bitcoin miningView →](https://256foundation.org/projects#mujina)

[SoftwareHydrapoolOne-click open-source mining poolView →](https://256foundation.org/projects#hydrapool)

Together, the four form the Development Kit.

[See the mining stack →](https://256foundation.org/projects)

They run.

In 2025, miners around the world pointed hashrate at our pool and found Bitcoin block 881423. Nine months later, our four projects ran together as a complete open-source mining kit. Real work, in public.

## We fund the builders.

Grants for open-source Bitcoin mining work. Money from anyone, influence from no one.

## A community, in code and out of it.

The forum is where the work gets argued out. The group chat is where it starts. Builders, miners, and advocates, worldwide.
