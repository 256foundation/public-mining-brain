# To decentralize Bitcoin mining by building, funding and stewarding open-source alternatives to every closed layer of the mining stack, so that the technology Bitcoin depends on cannot be owned, switched off, or permissioned by anyone

Bitcoin mining does three jobs. It issues new coins, settles transactions, and secures the record. All three matter to everyone who holds bitcoin.

Today, that machinery is closed. One company controls most of the hardware and software. Closed means unauditable, unfixable, and permissioned.

To open it, you need the recipes. A miner is four unique building blocks: a hashboard, a control board, firmware, and a pool. The knowledge to build each was locked away.

So we wrote them down. We reverse-engineered every layer, published the designs as open source, and fund the work to commoditize them, so anyone can build, audit, and compete.

A mature mining industry: multi-vendor, accessible reference designs, auditable and modifiable open software - as building blocks for the whole stack.

Issuance, settlement, and record security are truly decentralized and permissionless.

Money from anyone, influence from no one. We take money from anyone; it has no impact on how we run the organization. No special treatment for any funder.

No obligation to capture value. Our non-profit structure removes the incentive to capture value, which is why we can be the neutral home for the ecosystem’s shared dependencies, and never compete with the builders and companies that contribute.

We started the projects, we don’t own them, and we don’t sell them. Every core project and grant we fund is released under a recognized open-source licence, no exceptions.
