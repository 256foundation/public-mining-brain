### Product identity & naming
- Braiins OS is "an advanced autotuning firmware that optimizes the performance of your ASIC miners by automatically finding the optimal frequency and voltage for each individual chip, giving more work to high quality chips and less to low quality chips, to achieve a more efficient Watts per Terahash per second (W/TH/s) output at any power consumption level." - Release naming: "The latest version of Braiins OS can be identified by the dates following the prefix, which are formatted as YY-MM for major releases. For example, a release of Braiins OS occuring in October 2021 would be named as Braiins OS 21.10." [landing FAQ; "occuring" sic, verbatim]
- Version-behavior splits in docs reference 22.08.1 / 23.01 / 23.04 / 24.09 / 24.12 / 25.01 / 25.11 / 26.01 — current generation is 26.x. [dps, installation, technical-resources]

### Autotuning
- "Braiins OS is built to help miners realize the extra potential of their machines by improving the efficiency (W/TH) at any power level." [autotuning]
- Speed: "Machines can be tuned in about 10 minutes during which time the hashrate of the machine will fluctuate noticeably." … "Once the tuning is finished, the settings \"profile\" is saved, and you can return to it at any time in the future and immediately apply it without waiting for the machine to re-tune." [autotuning] (about page repeats: "Tuning takes about 10 minutes and only needs to run once per power target, the resulting profile is saved and can be reapplied instantly later without waiting for the miner to retune.")
- Landing FAQ gives a longer range: "The tuning usually takes between 1-9 hours to complete, depending on the type of control board, power limit specified and the quality of the chips in your device." [landing] — and the docs FAQ says "It depends on your device model and control board and can take several hours." [faq-dps-autotuning]
- Expectations: "Some machines will be in better condition… Those machines might see a 15%+ improvement in J/TH efficiency while others will only improve by 5% with the exact same configuration on Braiins OS." [autotuning]
- Per-chip definition: "Per-chip autotuning is the process of software calibrating the frequencies and voltages on every individual chip on a hash board to achieve higher efficiency." [faq-dps-autotuning]
- Hashboard power split: "Braiins OS assumes you have 3 hashboards and will take these values and apply 1/3 of the power limit to each hashboard. Even if a hashboard is disabled / removed / damaged, it will still apply 1/3 the power to each." [autotuning; nearly identical wording in faq-dps-autotuning]
- Tuner config location: "The tuner configuration is saved in /etc/bosminer-autotune.json" [faq-dps-autotuning]
- New in the 2026 docs: a (beta) **Continuous Tuner** — "instead of switching between fixed profiles, it keeps re-optimizing frequencies and voltages predictively every few minutes, and adapts automatically as chips degrade over time." [about; its own page /braiins-os/continuous-tuning was NOT captured (file cap)]

### DPS (Dynamic Performance Scaling)
- Definition: "Dynamic Performance Scaling (DPS) is designed to keep your miner operational & efficient when overheating… DPS dynamically adjusts power and hash-rate targets based on real-time conditions, helping miners maintain the ideal balance between performance and stability." [dps]
- Default state changed in 26.01 — verbatim table: "New installations: DPS is OFF by default (25.11 and Older) / DPS is ON by default (26.01 and Newer)"; "Upgraded machines: DPS settings are migrated from previous versions, On Start Target = 66%"; "On Start Target: When first enabled, DPS starts tuning on 66% from set Power Target (25.11-) / Newly installed machines start tuning on 100% of set Power Target (26.01+)". [dps]
- "In 26.01 and newer releases, DPS is on by default but will not be engaged if the miner is not overheated." If chip temp hits Hot "in the first 5 minutes of miner operations, it will downscale to 66% of the default Power Target". [dps]
- Downscale trigger: "Chip temperature is greater than or equal to the HOT temperature", downscale timeout 3 min, upscale timeout 30 s, never below the user-set minimal target. [dps]
- Upscale trigger (25.01+ Normal mode): "Chip temperature is less than or equal to the TARGET temperature"; Boost mode: "Chip temperature is at least 8°C below the HOT temperature"; plus stable-temp (≤1°C fluctuation/min), 1-hour overheating timeout, and (24.12-) fan speed below 100%. [dps]
- "DPS needs two conditions for upscaling: The miner's temperature is at least 5 degrees below the HOT limit and the fans are running below 80%." [faq-dps-autotuning]
- Pre-tuned profile ladder: "All profiles that are tuned by DPS are saved and do not need retuning… This way miners can tune approximately 6 - 15 targets, depending on what step size you are using." [dps]
- DPS Memory (anti-cycling): "If the miner scales up power and then quickly downscales within 30 minutes, DPS takes note of the conditions… The DPS memory automatically clears itself after 32 hours if no triggering event occurs." [dps]
- Grid Alignment snaps On-Start targets to the step grid (example: 66% of 3500 W = 2310 W → snapped to 2300 W). [dps]
- Modes: "DPS offers two modes: **NORMAL** and **BOOST**… **BOOST Mode:** The miner upscales more aggressively, continuing until it reaches 8°C below the HOT temperature threshold… this mode can increase the stress on the hardware at the cost of more hash-rate, proceed at your own risk!" [dps]
- Ramping for curtailment: "**Pause:** ~2 seconds / **Resume:** ~2 seconds". [about]
