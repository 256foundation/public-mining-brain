# Description

> Vnish by Hashcore — an alternative firmware with an Auto-tune function, designed to optimize the performance of Bitmain Antminer ASIC miners. The firmware unlocks the full potential of the hardware by increasing Hashrate, stability, and energy efficiency, which directly impacts long-term profitability.

The firmware functions as a full-fledged operating system: it manages hardware components, operating modes, and ensures a stable connection to mining Pools.

The firmware provides advanced low-level control over the hardware, eliminating the limitations of standard software in the areas of Overclocking, Undervolting, and device fleet management. The Auto-tune algorithm individually selects the frequency and voltage for each chip, ensuring an optimal ratio of Hashrate and power consumption (W/TH/s).
