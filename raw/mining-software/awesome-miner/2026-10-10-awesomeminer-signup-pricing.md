## Awesome Miner Antminer Firmware
Use the optimized Awesome Miner Antminer firmware to get significant hashrate improvements and more features. The firmware edition with a 2.8% mining fee doesn't consume any license in Awesome Miner. This makes it possible to run Awesome Miner and Cloud Services without paying anything except the 2.8% mining fee.
