Features

## Complete Mining Fleet Management,

Purpose-Built for Scale

A unified mining management software platform to monitor, automate, and optimize large-scale mining operations. Get real-time visibility, execute fleet-wide actions safely, visualize performance across sites and track assets through their lifecycle. Maximize profitability with Intelligent Miner: built-in economic curtailment and Ancillary Services participation, connected to live hashprice and energy markets.

## Intelligent Mining

Put your fleet on autopilot by always using the most profitable power settings based on your miner mix and external factors like hashprice and energy prices. Intelligent Miner runs natively on Commander, with built-in support for economic curtailment and Ancillary Services participation

## Real-Time Fleet Monitoring

Monitor hashrate, power consumption, efficiency, and temperature for every miner across all sites. Advanced filtering, saved views, and CSV export let you find issues before they cost you money.

## One-Click Bulk Actions

Change pool settings, adjust ATM tuning profiles, reboot, sleep, or wake thousands of miners in a single operation. Configurable batch sizes and delays protect your infrastructure during fleet-wide changes.

## Visual Sitemap & Heatmaps

See your entire facility as a rack-and-stack layout with heatmap overlays for temperature, hashrate, and power. Spot underperforming rows at a glance and drill down to the individual miner.

## Inventory & Asset Tracking

Track inventory assets through their full lifecycle. Smart tracking prevents losing track of assets after swapping PCB or hashboards. Associate metadata to the inventory assets from ERPs and 3rd party MMS providers.

## Automation Rules

Set condition-based rules that fire automatically like curtailing or rebooting a miner when temperature exceeds thresholds. Supports multi-site triggers with configurable evaluation frequency and stop conditions.

How it works

## Up and Running in 4 Steps

## 1

## Create a Site

Define a Site in Commander to model your physical facility — whether it's a single warehouse, a container farm, or a multi-building campus. Sites are the top-level entity that organizes everything: agents, miners, sitemaps, and automation rules.

## 2

## Install Agents

Deploy one or more lightweight agents for your site. Each agent scans its local network segment, discovers miners, and begins streaming real-time telemetry to Commander. Multiple agents let you cover different VLANs or network segments within the same facility. Works with LuxOS and stock firmware.

## 3

## Configure Your Facility

Import or build your sitemap layout, assign miners to racks, set up automated triggers and import your inventory metadata from external providers.

## 4

## Monitor, Automate, Optimize

View real-time fleet health across all sites, execute bulk actions with one click, let triggers handle routine optimization and Intelligent Miner run your fleet on Autopilot while you focus on scaling your operation.

## What's Next for Commander

IoT Devices Integration

Go beyond miner-only management. Connect PDUs, containers, smart switches, and other infrastructure devices to Commander for unified monitoring, remote power cycling, demand response curtailment, and circuit load balancing — all from the same dashboard you use to manage your fleet.

Ticketing System

Track issues from detection to resolution without leaving Commander. Tickets link directly to inventory assets and sitemap locations, so your operations team always knows which device is affected, where it sits in the facility, and its full repair history.

AI-Powered Operational Insights

Let Commander surface what matters most. AI-driven analysis across your fleet will identify underperforming miners, predict failures before they happen, and recommend actionable optimizations — turning your telemetry data into continuous performance gains without manual investigation.
