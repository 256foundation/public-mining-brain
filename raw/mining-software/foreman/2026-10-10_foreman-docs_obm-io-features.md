# Foreman Features

Managing a mining operation is high stakes.

Even small performance dips can result in significant cost increases. Our comprehensive [#essentials](Essentials) package offers all the tools you need to manage your operations efficiently and profitably. For more advanced options, select what you need from our [#premium](Premium) add-on features.

 [https://obm.io/contact/](Get Started)

## Essential Features

## 

 Real-Time Monitoring & Insights

Access a customizable dashboard for real-time visibility into key mining metrics such as hash rate and miner status. Foreman provides automated earnings tracking and reporting, enabling data-driven decisions to maximize profitability.

- Manage operations from one central platform with accurate, real-time tracking

- Stay ahead of potential issues through real-time monitoring, reporting, and automated alerts

- Track earnings and power usage all in one place

## 

 Miner Management & Configuration

Foreman makes it easy to oversee and manage your fleet, streamlining worker and pool configurations, site visualizations, and ticket tracking.

- Apply updates across dozens or thousands of miners simultaneously

- Visualize and manage miners and associated equipment for efficient and effective equipment tracking using our interactive Site Map

- Create tickets for equipment issues, track repair status and access repair history

## 

 Issue Detection & Resolution

Foreman enables proactive automation to adjust power modes, reset fans, or reboot miners, enabling you to identify and resolve potential problems quickly to minimize downtime and optimize efficiency.

- Detect performance issues in real time

- Receive customizable real-time alerts for critical events such as miner downtime, power outages, and pool issues, through platforms like Discord, Telegram, Google Chat, and Slack

- Automate miner-focused actions, including rebooting or changing pools

## 

 Infrastructure Integration & Monitoring

Integrate your infrastructure and auxiliary devices to access real-time stats , allowing you to monitor power, network performance, and temperatures.

- Smart PDUs & Phase Balancing — Monitor each miner’s consumption directly from the PDU port and perform hard reboots through your dashboard

- Cooling Towers — Manage temperatures, monitor alarm status and review flow data

- External Fans and Temperature Readers — Gain detailed insights into your operating environment for improved control and efficiency

- Network Switches and IP-Based Mapping — Monitor switches and create a site map of your networking equipment within minutes

## 

 Power Controls

Effective energy management is essential for mining success. Manage energy usage to enhance performance and reduce operational costs.

- Schedule power adjustments, regulate speed, and control energy consumption

- Set customizable rules to respond instantly to events such as time of day, miner temperatures, or profitability

- Automatically adjust miners to keep them profitable

## Premium Features

Maximize the value of your mining investment. Foreman’s premium add-ons give you the tools to optimize performance, reduce costs, and boost profitability.

## 

 Advanced Power Management

Foreman provides hands-free, automated energy management for operations of any size and market— whether operating in regulated or deregulated markets.

## Price Response Plus

Maximize profitability at every price point in dynamic pricing markets by having Foreman calculate model-specific strike prices in real-time or defining your own. Three curtailment strategies allow you to apply the best strategy to each of your miners based on each miner’s hardware and firmware.

- Basic — Delivers reliable on/off control when strike price thresholds are met to avoid high costs

- Power Mode — Adds discrete states to underclock or overclock your mines to generative savings or capitalize on lower energy costs

- Wattage Target — Enables granular, wattage-level precision to capture profit at price points that simpler strategies miss entirely

[https://obm.io/blog/price-response-plus/](Learn More)

## Partner Integrations

Capture additional revenue streams and reduce costs by integrating with your power partner to automate the execution of your power strategy with programs like Demand Response, Peak Avoidance, etc.

- Third-Party Integration — Out-of-the-box integrations with leading QSEs, CSPs, local utilities, and co-ops

- Earn Revenue or Savings — Get paid for curtailment in deregulated markets or secure lower energy rates in regulated areas. Foreman supports leading Demand Response, Peak Response, and other programs

- Automatic Participation — Aggregators such as Voltus, CPower, and GridBeyond manage the process so you can stay focused on daily operations

## Adaptive dispatches

Whether curtailing for CP events, operating in compliance with your time of use agreement, or leveraging behind-the-meter power generation, Adaptive Dispatches deliver maximum profit per MW while protecting critical infrastructure.

- Active Curtailment Monitoring – Ensure that curtailed miners stay down for the duration of a dispatch

- Set Point Dispatches – Keep your power draw below your allowed target

- Buffered Dispatches – Maximize your use of [https://obm.io/blog/obm-launches-behind-the-meter-load-management-solution/](behind-the meter power generation) (solar, wind, gas flare, etc.)

- Recurring Schedules – Schedule curtailments to perform when needed without all the stress

- Execution Sequences – Mine only your most profitable miners when you curtail part of your load

## 

 Foreman Intelligence

Portfolio analytics built for leadership. No data analyst required.

Running a Bitcoin mining operation at scale means managing thousands of assets, dozens of data streams, and a market that moves fast. Most organizations are doing it with spreadsheets and an overworked analyst.

Foreman Intelligence, an advanced analytics platform, combines Foreman’s miner management data and real-time inputs, e.g. LMP prices, hash price, and market data, delivering a unified view across all sites. Powerful yet intuitive, it enables more effective fleet management, reducing costs and boosting profits.

- For Finance — Cost-per-coin, power cost variance, curtailment P&L, and hardware efficiency trending, on demand and audit-ready.

- For Operations — Hash rate, uptime, fleet status, and cross-site failure patterns rolled up consistently across every site, in one view.

- For Executives — Portfolio-level profitability, revenue, and net income in a single executive view, built for leadership conversations, not operator workflows.
