To retrieve your Foreman API credentials, follow these steps:

- Navigate to the Man with A Tie icon located in the top right corner of your dashboard

- Select My Account from the dropdown menu

- Scroll down to the API Information section below your Account Information

- Here you will find:

- Your unique API Key

- Your Client ID

These credentials are required for configuring your Pickaxe installation and connecting your mining hardware to your Foreman dashboard.
