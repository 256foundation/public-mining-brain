# Bitcoin Miner Solutions

More uptime. Lower costs. Less collateral.

OBM gives Bitcoin miners the tools to simplify operations, optimize energy usage, and reduce financial and operational risk, so you can focus on growing your hash rate instead of managing complexity.

Our solutions, Foreman and OBM Vantage, work together to cover both sides of your operation. Foreman gives you the automation, insights, and precision to run your mine with confidence: monitoring performance, managing power, and catching problems before they cost you uptime. OBM Vantage gives you the visibility, precision, and control you need to provide less collateral, settle contracts daily, and act on costs while they’re still accruing.

Trusted by more than 10,000 customers, OBM helps miners scale with confidence, control costs, and unlock new revenue opportunities, all while staying agile enough to react when the market shifts.

## Command your mine from anywhere.

Foreman is the industry-leading bitcoin mine management software, trusted by more than 10,000 customers to remotely manage sites of any size. Foreman gives operators the automation, insights, and precision they need to optimize efficiency, boost profitability, and reduce operational risk, so you can scale with confidence and control costs.

## Simplify Mine Management

- Manage operations from one central platform with real-time monitoring of hash rates, energy consumption, equipment health, and asset configuration

- Reboot idle miners, reassign equipment for repairs, and resolve issues before they cost you uptime

- Apply updates across dozens or thousands of miners simultaneously

## Optimize Energy Consumption

- Automate power decisions with hands-free energy management built for regulated and deregulated markets alike

- Curtail, slow down, or overclock mines automatically based on real-time pricing to protect margins

- Capture additional revenue by integrating directly with leading QSEs, aggregators, and utilities

## Boost Profitability

- Track earnings and power usage in one place with automated reporting built for data-driven decisions

- Scale visibility and control as your operation grows, without adding headcount

- Reduce downtime and operational risk with proactive alerts and issue detection

## Turn Data Into Decisions

Foreman Intelligence gives you portfolio-level visibility into your operation, no data analyst required

- Access detailed datasets and build custom reports tailored to your specific needs

- Drill into performance metrics at any interval to uncover trends before they cost you money

- Give Finance and Operations leaders the same source of truth, so decisions are backed by data

## Manage your power costs while you can still make an impact.

OBM Vantage is a real-time energy analytics platform, putting measured load, live tariff pricing, and daily settlement in one place. It empowers large energy consumers with the visibility, precision, and control you need to provide less collateral, settle contracts daily, and act on costs while they’re still accruing.

Three products, one platform: Vantage Insights, Vantage Settlements, and Vantage Direct Metering.
