## Item 1 Business — strategy (VERBATIM excerpts)

- Positioning: "CleanSpark is a data center developer, until recently focused exclusively on bitcoin mining. We independently own, lease and operate a large portfolio of data centers and power assets across the United States with locations in Georgia, Tennessee, Mississippi and Wyoming for a total contracted power capacity of approximately 1,027 megawatts ("MW") as of September 30, 2025."
- AI/HPC pivot: "Our data centers are also well-suited to support hyperscale cloud providers, AI providers, and various other technology companies. We are in the process of developing our customer base in relation to our HPC and AI services business through our data centers."
- Texas entry (subsequent to FY end): "at the end of October 2025, we announced that we acquired rights to approximately 271 acres of land in Austin County, Texas and executed long-term power supply agreements totaling 285 megawatts to support the development of a next-generation data center campus. We believe this transaction positions us to deliver scalable, resilient, and energy-efficient capacity to meet accelerating demand from AI, cloud, and enterprise workloads." (MD&A: "This transaction marked the Company's entry into the Texas market and expanded our power portfolio for future AI and HPC development.")
- AI/HPC revenue status: "As of the date of filing of this Annual Report on Form 10-K, we have not had material revenue from our AI and HPC services business, and operating results from this business are not reflected in historical results of operations."
- Treasury/trading: "In April 2025, we launched our institutional-grade in-house trading function as we shift to a balanced approach between monetizing new production and building long-term holdings... we began entering into bitcoin-linked derivative contracts to economically hedge the volatility of bitcoin prices..."

## BTC holdings & treasury (Notes + MD&A)

- "As of September 30, 2025 and 2024, the Company held 10,428 and 6,819 bitcoin, respectively." Holdings table: "Cost basis - per bitcoin $105,025; Fair value - per bitcoin $114,068; Cost basis of bitcoin (in '000s) $1,095,151; Fair value of bitcoin (in '000s) $1,189,443".
- "As of September 30, 2025, the Company held approximately 10,428 bitcoin on hand and the equivalent of 2,583 bitcoin receivable as a result of collateral held by third parties... the fair value of the Company's bitcoin holdings and receivable from collateral on that date to approximate $1,189 and $295 million, respectively."
- Digital Asset Management (DAM) strategy: "During the year ended September 30, 2025, the Company instituted a Digital Asset Management ("DAM") treasury strategy designed to enhance liquidity and generate incremental income... primarily covered call and put option contracts, that are collateralized by bitcoin held in treasury... These activities provided approximately $12.1 million of proceeds from premiums and incremental Spot+ trading during the fiscal year... The Company had no DAM derivative positions open as of September 30, 2025."
- Strategic reserve: "In March 2025, the Company made a strategic decision to change its bitcoin treasury policy and determined that a portion of its bitcoin holdings would be held long-term... The noncurrent portion represents bitcoin that the Company intends to hold for the long-term as part of its strategic reserve."
- Bitmain bitcoin-for-miners deals: "In April and May 2025, we executed agreements with Bitmain Technologies Delaware Limited... to acquire new-generation miners under terms that allowed payment in bitcoin at negotiated premiums to prevailing market prices. As part of these transactions, we secured embedded options to repurchase the transferred bitcoin at the same premium prices..."
- Custody: "As of September 30, 2025, we held approximately 98% of our bitcoin in cold storage and 2% in hot wallets" (all via Coinbase).
- Credit facility: "On August 7, 2024, the Company signed a Master Loan Agreement (the "2024 Master Loan") with Coinbase Credit, Inc.... The 2024 Master Loan has a credit limit of $50,000 [thousands]... loan-to-value ratio... equal to or less than 64%."
