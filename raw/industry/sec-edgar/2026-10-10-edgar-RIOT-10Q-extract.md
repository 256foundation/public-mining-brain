## Strategy — data center / AI pivot (VERBATIM)

- "Riot Platforms, Inc. is a vertically integrated digital infrastructure company principally engaged in developing and optimizing its large-scale power assets. The Company's business strategy centers on enhancing its electrical infrastructure and deploying it across complementary platforms: (i) bitcoin mining and (ii) scalable data center solutions designed to support non-mining workloads."
- Strategy language in forward-looking statements: "the Company's strategic evolution from a bitcoin mining-focused enterprise to a diversified data center and digital infrastructure company."
- AMD Lease (Note 3): "In January 2026, the Company entered into the AMD Lease... at the Rockdale Facility. The AMD Lease included an initial deployment of 25 MW of critical IT load capacity delivered in phases beginning with 5 MW... delivered in January 2026 and the remaining 20 MW that was delivered in May 2026. The AMD Lease carries a term of 10 years, with three five-year extension options." Amendment (April 2026): "AMD exercised a portion of the existing expansion option... to provide an additional deployment of 25 MW of critical IT load capacity to be delivered in phases beginning with 10 MW... expected to be delivered in November 2026 and the remaining 15 MW expected to be delivered in May 2027... If both the remaining 50 MW of reserved capacity under the existing expansion option and the additional 100 MW option are fully exercised, AMD's total leased capacity at the Rockdale Facility would increase to 200 MW."
- TENANT LEASE (Note 3, subsequent to quarter end — the headline item): "In August 2026, the Company and a leading frontier AI lab, (the "Tenant"), entered into a long-term data center lease agreement (the "Tenant Lease") at the Rockdale Facility for an initial deployment of 191 MW of critical IT load capacity. ... The Tenant Lease includes an initial deployment of 96 MW of critical IT load capacity anticipated to be delivered in December 2027, with the remaining capacity anticipated to be delivered in June 2028. The Tenant Lease carries an initial term of 20 years, commencing upon full deployment anticipated in June 2028, includes provisions for two successive five-year renewal terms at the option of the lessee, and is expected to generate approximately $9.1 billion in base rent over the initial term." [XBRL context tag in the filing identifies the tenant as "LeaseAgreementWithAnthropicPbcMember"; the document text anonymizes the tenant.]
- $573M credit facility (Note 3): "In August 2026, Riot DC Logistics LLC... entered into a $573.0 million interim credit facility... with the lenders party thereto and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc., as administrative agent, to fund long-lead equipment procurement and other development costs for the Tenant Lease. The facility matures on October 15, 2026... bears interest at a variable rate of the Secured Overnight Financing Rate ("SOFR") plus 2.75%, and is secured by substantially all assets of the Borrower related to the project... non-recourse to Riot Platforms, Inc."
- New segment: "During the first quarter of 2026, the Company's execution of a significant data center lease with AMD resulted in material Data Center revenue... Accordingly, the Company's data center operations now meet the quantitative and qualitative requirements to be recognized as a separate reportable segment." (Three segments now: Bitcoin Mining, Data Centers, Engineering.)

## Facilities & fleet (Note 1 + MD&A "Operating Metrics")

- "The Rockdale Facility currently provides up to approximately 700 megawatts ("MW") of developed capacity for Bitcoin Mining and data center leasing. The Corsicana Facility is currently equipped to provide up to approximately 400 MW of developed capacity for Bitcoin Mining, and upon completion, is expected to have a total of approximately one gigawatt ("GW") of developed capacity available for high-density compute workloads. The Kentucky Facility currently provides approximately 192 MW of developed capacity."
- Deployed hash rate as of June 30, 2026: "Combined Bitcoin Mining hash rate, deployed: 44.4 EH/s" (Rockdale 17.4, Corsicana 16.6, Kentucky 10.5) vs 35.4 EH/s June 30, 2025.
- Average operating hash rate Q2 2026: combined 37.2 EH/s (vs 31.7 Q2'25); 6M 36.8 EH/s (vs 30.8) — "+19.5%".
- Total power capacity: 1,292 MW (Rockdale 700, Corsicana 400, Kentucky 192) vs 1,165 MW a year earlier.
- All-in power cost (cents/kWh): Q2'26 combined 3.6¢ (Rockdale 3.5, Corsicana 3.2, Kentucky 5.2); 6M'26 3.3¢.
