## Item 1 Business — strategy

- Two platforms (VERBATIM): "We are a vertically integrated digital infrastructure company principally engaged in developing and optimizing our large-scale power assets. Our business centers on enhancing our electrical infrastructure and deploying it across two complementary platforms: (i) Bitcoin Mining and (ii) scalable data center solutions designed to support non-mining workloads."
- Segments: "We operate in two reportable business segments: Bitcoin Mining and Engineering." (Former Data Center Hosting segment: "In 2023, we made the decision to stop pursuing new bitcoin mining hosting contracts and end our legacy contracts... During the year ended December 31, 2024, all agreements with legacy Data Center Hosting bitcoin mining customers were terminated.")
- Data center / AI pivot (Item 1, "Data Center Development"): "In 2025, we began leveraging our core competencies in power optimization, strategic land acquisition, engineering design, and construction execution to begin actively pursuing opportunities to develop and monetize portions of our existing facilities and power pipeline through the provision of data center leasing services." "We have completed our basis of design for our standard data center build and are assessing the procurement of all long-lead equipment..."
- AMD lease (Item 1 + MD&A): "in January 2026, we announced the execution of a long-term data center lease agreement (the "AMD Lease") with Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. ("AMD"), to initially provide 25 MW of critical IT load capacity at the Rockdale Facility. The AMD Lease has an initial term of ten years and provides for expansion options for up to an additional 75 MW of critical IT load capacity, and a right of first refusal of up to an additional 100 MW. Furthermore, the AMD Lease includes provisions for three successive five-year renewal terms at the option of the lessee."
- Rockdale land purchase (Jan 2026): "the fee simple acquisition of the approximately 200-acre parcel of land underlying the Rockdale Facility ... including its 700 MW grid interconnection, dedicated water supply, and redundant fiber connectivity."
- Industry trend framing (Item 1, "Industry Trends"): "A notable emerging trend is the convergence of bitcoin mining operations with large scale data center services, including those supporting AI/HPC workloads."

## Fleet, sites & capacity (Item 1 + Item 7 "Bitcoin Mining Metrics")

- Hash rate: "As of December 31, 2025, our Bitcoin Mining business segment had a total deployed hash rate of 38.5 exahash per second ("EH/s"), as compared to a total deployed hash rate of 31.5 EH/s as of December 31, 2024." Deployed by site: Rockdale 16.4 EH/s, Corsicana 15.7 EH/s, Kentucky 6.4 EH/s.
- Average operating hash rate FY2025: combined 31.4 EH/s (Rockdale 13.7, Corsicana 14.2, Kentucky 3.5) vs 15.0 EH/s FY2024.
- Developed power capacity: "Total power capacity 1,292 MW" (Rockdale 700 MW, Corsicana 400 MW, Kentucky 192 MW) vs 1,160 MW in 2024. Kentucky targeted "to reach a total capacity of approximately 232 MW through the remainder of 2026."
- Corsicana buildout: "currently has 400 MW of developed capacity and, upon completion, is expected to have a total of approximately one gigawatt ("GW") of developed capacity available for data center workloads."
- All-in power cost (cents/kWh) FY2025: combined 3.7¢ (Rockdale 3.7¢, Corsicana 3.5¢, Kentucky 4.8¢) vs 3.4¢ FY2024.
- Miner pipeline: "we executed purchase orders under the Master Agreement [with MicroBT] to acquire miners with a total hash rate of 49.2 EH/s, for a total purchase price of approximately $779.5 million... All miners under current purchase orders are expected to be received by the second quarter of 2026."
- Power strategy: ERCOT Demand Response curtailment; "Power curtailment credits are credited against our power invoices as a result of temporarily pausing our operations to participate in ERCOT's Demand Response Service Programs."

## MD&A — cost to mine & results (Item 7)

- Production: "we mined 5,686 bitcoin, as compared to 4,828 bitcoin mined during the year ended December 31, 2024" (+858 BTC, +17.8%).
- Cost to mine table FY2025: "Cost of power for self-mining operations $281,396" (thousands); "Cost of revenue for self-mining operations, net of power curtailment credits, excluding bitcoin miner depreciation $282,282"; "Less: power curtailment credits $(56,729)"; "Bitcoin miner depreciation $237,574"; "Production value of one bitcoin mined $101,350" (vs $66,488); "Cost to mine one bitcoin, excluding bitcoin miner depreciation $49,645" (49.0% of production value); "Cost to mine one bitcoin, including bitcoin miner depreciation $91,427" (90.2% of production value).
- Revenue FY2025 (income statement): "Bitcoin Mining $576,276; Engineering $64,688; Other $6,471; Total revenue $647,435" (thousands) vs FY2024: 321,002 / 38,491 / 17,165. ("For the year ended December 31, 2025, Bitcoin Mining revenue was approximately $576.3 million.")
- Net loss: "we reported a net loss of $663.2 million" FY2025 (net loss $(663,181)K vs net income $109,401K FY2024); basic EPS $(1.95).
- Impairment: "The impairment of property and equipment during the years ended December 31, 2025 and 2024 was $29.7 million and $0.0 million... from certain long-lead items previously included in Construction in progress related to the planned expansion of the Corsicana Facility for bitcoin mining purposes. These items were deemed to be impaired as a result of our decision to expand the Corsicana Facility for data center application purposes instead."
- Liquidity: "our net working capital totaled approximately ($21.1) million, including cash and cash equivalents of $233.5 million."
- Miner purchases: "we paid cash of approximately $228.4 million... in total deposits and payments for the purchase of miners" FY2025.
