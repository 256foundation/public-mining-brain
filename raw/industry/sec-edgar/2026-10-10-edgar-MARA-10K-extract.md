## Item 1 Business — strategy

AI/HPC pivot language (VERBATIM, Item 1, "2025 Developments"):
> "Additionally, as part of this expansion, we entered into a strategic agreement (the "Strategic Agreement") with Starwood Digital Ventures ("Starwood") to develop, finance and operate AI and HPC infrastructure on select power-rich sites within our existing portfolio. Under the Strategic Agreement, we will contribute certain sites to and retain up to a 50% ownership interest in a newly formed joint venture, while Starwood will lead engineering, procurement and construction activities, secure hyperscale tenancy and operate the assets."
> "The joint platform is expected to provide more than 1 GW of initial IT capacity in the initial development, with a potential pathway to expand to more than 2.5 GW over time."

Risk-factor framing (Item 1A): "While we intend to continue our Bitcoin mining operations, the allocation of resources to support AI and HPC development may reduce the capital, personnel, infrastructure and power capacity available for our mining business."

## Fleet & capacity (Item 1 "Operations" + MD&A)

- "As of December 31, 2025, we operated approximately 490,000 mining rigs globally, including our proportionate share of mining rigs attributable to our equity method investee, the Abu Dhabi Global Markets company (the "ADGM Entity"), with an energized hashrate of approximately 66.4 exahashes per second ("EH/s")." (Item 1)
- Capacity table (Item 1, "Operations"): Total 1,315 MW operational capacity / 1,861 MW total capacity / 66.4 EH/s energized hashrate. Owned sites 779 MW op / 1,324 MW total / 37.2 EH/s. Hosted sites 536 MW op / 537 MW total / 29.2 EH/s.
- Owned breakdown (MW op / MW total / EH/s): Central Texas 411/550/21.8; East Ohio 79/225/4.0; North Texas 78/180/2.4; Central Ohio 28/150/1.3; Central Nebraska 127/142/5.5; International 57/77/2.3.
- Hosted breakdown: Central North Dakota 273 MW / 16.6 EH/s; West Texas 216 MW / 10.2 EH/s; Other 48 MW / 2.4 EH/s. (Item 1: "these hosted sites collectively provided approximately 537 MW of total capacity and 29.2 EH/s of energized hashrate.")
- Sites: "Our Granbury, Texas site supports approximately 300 megawatt ("MW") of total capacity and is one of the largest containerized liquid immersion-cooled sites worldwide... As of December 31, 2025, Granbury had an energized hashrate of 12.3 EH/s." (Item 1)
- Wind farm: "We acquired a wind farm in Hansford County, Texas, with 240 MW of interconnection capacity and 114 MW of nameplate wind capacity to establish a behind-the-meter data center at low energy costs." (Item 1 highlights; MD&A: acquired Feb 2025 for $49.2M total consideration; investing cash out $36.4M "to acquire the Wind Farm for an additional 114 megawatts of nameplate capacity")
- Meerkat Acquisition (Central Nebraska): "Subsequent to year end, in January 2026, we increased our footprint in Nebraska through an acquisition of a 42 MW of total capacity data center adjacent to an existing site, expanding our Nebraska campus by approximately 40%." (MD&A; closed Jan 21, 2026 for $25.0M cash, Note 3)
- Energy sensitivity: "As of December 31, 2025, we had approximately 779 megawatts of operational capacity at our owned sites, and approximately 536 megawatts of allocated capacity under contract at third-party hosted sites... a hypothetical $10 per megawatt hour change in the price of electricity would cause cash flows to vary by approximately $115.2 million." (Item 7A)

## Balance sheet / BTC holdings

- "As of December 31, 2025, we held approximately 53,822 bitcoin, including 15,315 bitcoin under our digital asset management strategy, on our Consolidated Balance Sheets, with a carrying value of approximately $4.7 billion." (MD&A)
- "approximately 9,377 bitcoin were loaned to third parties to generate additional returns and approximately 5,938 bitcoin were pledged as collateral for outstanding borrowings" (Item 1)
- "the remaining 38,507 unrestricted bitcoin were classified as long-term assets under "Digital assets, net of current portion"... with a fair value of $3.4 billion." (MD&A)
- "we sold approximately 4,076 bitcoin for $413.1 million" FY2025; strategy changed 2H2025 to permit sales of mined BTC, expanded 2026 to allow selling balance-sheet BTC. (MD&A)
- Loss on digital assets: "We recognized a loss on digital assets of $304.6 million for the year ended December 31, 2025 compared to a gain of $813.8 million in the prior year period."
- Sensitivity: "a hypothetical $10,000 change in the market price of bitcoin would have resulted in an estimated change of $538.2 million... to our income (loss) before income taxes."
- Liquidity: "Our combined cash and cash equivalents, excluding restricted cash and digital assets, including bitcoin under our digital asset management strategy, totaled $5.3 billion as of December 31, 2025."
