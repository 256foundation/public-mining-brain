## Overview

OCEAN is a non-custodial Bitcoin mining pool built around two in-house designs: the TIDES reward system and the DATUM template protocol. It is operated by "Bitcoin Ocean, LLC, a subsidiary of Mummolin, Inc., a Wyoming corporation", and advertises SOC 1 and SOC 2 attestations — the homepage links a press release headlined OCEAN Earns SOC 1 and SOC 2 Type 2 Attestations, "Bringing Independent Assurance to Permissionless Bitcoin Mining" ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md)). Scale, self-reported: the homepage banner at capture read "HASHRATE: 21.74 Eh/s • LAST BLOCK: 970835 (4H AGO)" and the docs-index header the same day showed "HASHRATE: 21.49 Eh/s, LAST BLOCK: 970835 (3H AGO)" (both As-Of 2026-10-10) ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md); [docs index](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-index.md)). On mempool.space's trailing-week ranking OCEAN is ninth with 29 of 1072 blocks and a 92.61% average block match rate — the lowest match rate in the top ten (As-Of 2026-10-10) ([mempool.space 1w](../../raw/pools/mempool/2026-10-10-mempool-pool-share-1w.md)).

The pool's pitch is "Take control of your bitcoin mining and make more money", with miner testimonials on the homepage — including the line most often quoted by its users: "No KYC, only payout address." Setup requires only a stratum endpoint and a payout address: "no account required, just point your miners to mine.ocean.xyz:3334" ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md)).

Payouts run on TIDES — the pool's own Transparent Index of Distinct Extended Shares — and access to block-template control runs through DATUM, after "We decommissioned our alternate template options on December 21st, 2025." — the pre-DATUM menu of OCEAN-hosted template policies (OCEAN Recommended / Core+Antispam / Core / Data-Free, each at a 2% fee) is preserved only as a starting point for self-configured templates ([alternate templates](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-alternate-templates.md)). Unlike the other majors in this loop (Foundry, AntPool, F2Pool, ViaBTC), OCEAN's commercial terms are stated twice over: in marketing and again in a binding ToS ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md); [ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).

## Fees and payout

- **Fee: 2% standard, 1% with DATUM.** Homepage: "2% fee — 1% fee for miners who make their own blocks using DATUM", also phrased as DATUM users enjoying a "50% discount on pool fees". The ToS makes it contractual: "The standard fee for Services rendered is 2% of all Bitcoin network rewards and transaction fees per block found with help from OCEAN", less any incentives or promotions in effect when the block is found; after a Chain Split the fee applies separately per Supported Chain (As-Of 2026-10-10) ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md); [ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md); [Origins of DATUM](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-datum.md)).
- **TIDES, in one line (pool's own abstract):** "TIDES is a Bitcoin mining pool payout system which reduces payout variance as low as possible without the pool operator needing to hold and buffer the Bitcoin rewards as a custodian." ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md))
- **Mechanics.** The share-log window is "eight (8) blocks worth of work at the current Bitcoin network difficulty"; every accepted proof is indexed individually in order, and "Shares are never removed from the share log." When a block is found, "The reward is then split up proportionally based on their percentage of the shares in the top of the share log window" — the full "total block reward (subsidy plus transaction fees)" — and at that window size "all shares submitted have a 99.9665% chance of being rewarded at least once", with shares rewarded eight times on average. The homepage FAQ states the contrast plainly: "Unlike PPLNS or FPPS, TIDES does not merely approximate reward distributions, so miners are paid the full and exact pro rata portion of their contributed hashrate." ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md); [homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md))
- **Fees at share resolution.** TIDES tags fees per share: "Every share is tagged with its fee rate flag at the time it is mined", and the design explicitly supports differential pricing, making it easy "for a pool to encourage decentralized mining by offering a lower fee for shares found using such protocols" — which is how the 1% DATUM rate is implemented ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md)).
- **Anti-FPPS positioning, verbatim.** "Unlike hashrate-rental payout systems such as FPPS, TIDES can only reward miners with Bitcoin that is mined."; "TIDES is what PPLNS was originally supposed to be."; and, conceding a limit: "TIDES, by nature of how the share log works, cannot itself be decentralized, sadly." The homepage adds that unlike FPPS, TIDES "does not require the pool to serve as a custodial intermediary for payment processing" ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md); [homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md)).
- **Thresholds.** The homepage FAQ states the on-chain threshold as "0.01048576 BTC or 1,048,576 sats" (automatic payout above it); discretionary payouts for balances above 0.00065536 BTC if you stop mining or change address, "should it be practical to do so"; Lightning payouts can land below the threshold (As-Of 2026-10-10) ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md)). TIDES itself is threshold-free — "TIDES itself has no concept of a minimum payout" is the doc's framing; thresholds are a pool-level payout policy ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md)).
- **Variance, by design.** Because the pool does not buffer funds, earnings arrive only when the pool finds blocks, and new miners face a ramp: "a brand new miner who joins the pool might not earn any reward for a block" found in their first moments. After quitting, "you will typically continue to receive Payouts, less Total Fees, for an average of eight solved blocks following your voluntary termination" ([TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md); [ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).
- **TIDES-only entitlement.** "Nothing in this Agreement entitles any User to any payout method, schedule, or settlement term other than TIDES." Custom payout arrangements are possible but confidential and discretionary ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).

## Custody and access

- **Non-custodial, coinbase-direct.** The homepage feature block: "Get newly generated bitcoins directly from the network"; the FAQ: "OCEAN constructs blocks so that miners are paid in the generation transaction whenever possible." The ToS agrees: "rather than take custody of your expected Bitcoin earnings like many other pools" — "OCEAN remains non-custodial" ([homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md); [ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).
- **But sub-threshold balances are contractually OCEAN's.** ToS §3(a): amounts below the on-chain threshold are "a contingent contractual entitlement to future Payouts only", and "you hold no property interest in, or claim to, any specific bitcoin" until a payout is made. Lightning payouts are an optional, revocable convenience — "OCEAN may suspend, modify, limit, or discontinue any optional payout feature at any time" — and OCEAN "does not permit Users to open Lightning channels directly with OCEAN" ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).
- **No KYC; permissionless by design.** The ToS: "you do not and cannot create an account or reveal customer or personally identifiable User information as a condition of use", only a Bitcoin address — with the warning "Do not use a Bitcoin address with OCEAN that you do not have the private keys for". Users must represent they are not sanctioned (US/UN/EU/UK, including OFAC), but there is no conventional identity collection; the homepage FAQ compresses it to "No KYC, only payout address." ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md); [homepage](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-home.md))
- **Privacy posture.** "OCEAN does not fingerprint browsers. Cookies are not logged"; the optional Portal app is end-to-end encrypted — "OCEAN holds no decryption key and does not read it" ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).
- **Chain-split policy (2026 ToS addition).** OCEAN may support multiple chains after a Protocol Event; "Which chain your hashrate works on is determined solely by your own configuration — your selection of a Stratum endpoint, or the software, consensus rules, and block templates of your own DATUM node." Split-chain "rewards not spendable for ≥100 blocks"; unpaid balances on discontinued chains are extinguished; and "Nothing in this Section 22 creates any trust, escrow, bailment, agency, fiduciary, or debtor-creditor relationship." ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md))
- **Operational fine print.** ToS §15 disclaims uptime guarantees and recommends failover: "OCEAN recommends you keep your miners configured with a failover/fallback Service (including solo mining) to avoid any potential loss as a result of downtime" Liability is capped at the greater of fees retained in the prior ninety days or "ONE HUNDRED UNITED STATES DOLLARS ($100)"; disputes are "governed in all respects by the laws of the State of Wyoming" with "The Wyoming Chancery Court shall be the exclusive venue" ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).

## SV2 / DATUM

- **DATUM, not Stratum V2 — and the pool says why.** "Initially, we considered Stratum V2 (Sv2), a protocol that promised to address some of these issues." but it "became clear that Sv2 wouldn't be a viable solution in the near term", so OCEAN created DATUM, "a protocol built from the ground up", billed as "the first fully decentralized mining protocol since 2017 when the last decentralized pool shut down", with Sv2 dismissed as "bolted onto the original centralized design" ([Origins of DATUM](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-datum.md)).
- **What DATUM requires.** "To use DATUM, miners will need a DATUM gateway, which communicates directly with their mining hardware, and a Bitcoin full node to peer with the Bitcoin network and manage block submissions." DATUM "is currently in public beta"; the setup guide recommends Bitcoin Knots — "Bitcoin Knots as it has more template controls than CORE" — and has miners point at their own gateway on port 23334, with the usual OCEAN username (payout address, optional `.workername`) ([DATUM setup guide](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-datum-setup.md)).
- **Incentive alignment.** "DATUM miners receive a 50% discount on the OCEAN pool fee", and the TIDES doc explains the mechanism: per-share fee flags let one pool price centralized and decentralized templates differently ([Origins of DATUM](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-datum.md); [TIDES doc](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-tides.md)).
- **Non-custodial payout claim, verbatim:** "coinbase payouts go directly to miners, instantaneously and without custodial oversight" ([Origins of DATUM](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-datum.md)). Note the boundary case the ToS itself defines: below the payout threshold, the bitcoin is OCEAN's until payout (see Custody and access) ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)).
- **Stratum V2 naming.** The ToS treats the "block templates of your own DATUM node" as first-class configuration but never names Stratum V2 (capture note, As-Of 2026-10-10) ([ToS](../../raw/pools/ocean/2026-10-10-ocean-xyz-docs-terms.md)). For the wider debate — OCEAN as the standing Sv2 dissenter, and the custody critique of its payout path — see [Decentralized Pool Designs](../protocols/decentralized-pool-designs.md) and [Pool Centralization & FPPS](pool-centralization-and-fpps.md).
