## Specs (launch announcement, published 2025-10-29)

- Debut: "successfully debuted its groundbreaking Avalon A16 series (A16XP-300T, 12.8J/TH ±5%; A16-282T, 13.8J/TH ±5%) miner this week at the Blockchain Life 2025 event held in Dubai Festival City."
- "The centerpiece of Canaan's presence at Booth L15, the Avalon A16XP immediately captured industry attention with its powerful Hashrate of 300 TH/s."
- Confirmed block: Hash Rate "300 TH/s (with a tolerance of ±5% at 25°C)"; AC Voltage Input "220~277 Volts"; Working Temperature "-5 to 35°C"; Net Dimensions "366mm (L) * 213mm (W) * 300mm"; algorithm SHA256/BTC.
- Engineering claims: "an all-new fan array and wind-tunnel design" letting it "run markedly quieter" while inheriting "Avalon's renowned tolerance for extreme ambient temperatures".
- Gap: no wall-power figure on the launch page; the shop product page for A16XP-300T exists per Wayback URL inventory but was not captured (file cap).

- Air-cooled flagship (no hydro variant announced for the A16 generation as of the captures); 3850 W heat class with the new "fan array and wind-tunnel design" — quieter running per the launch claim, which matters for in-home placements.
