## Overview

Between 2024 and 2026 the Hashrate Heatpunks community went from one-off DIY rigs to a small but real vendor ecosystem: integrated hydro boilers built around the single-phase Whatsminer M64 (Heat Core HS05, RY3T Mini), immersion systems (Hashrate House, Fog Hashing, Constellation Heating), forced-air furnace replacements, water-heater retrofits (Superheat H1, Aqueon), and installer-operators such as Softwarm and Ecobit that heat car washes, concrete plants, hangars and warehouses. The durable lessons are commercial rather than technical: pick sites that already spend heavily on heat and can run year-round, move heat with fluid so the computer can live outside the occupied space, use listed components (PDU, pumps, PSUs) to get through inspection, and treat the heat dump as an optional feature rather than a requirement. Prices and specs below are dated by their anchors and are volatile.

## Rules of thumb for choosing a site

- **Follow the heat bill.** The best places to mine for heat are where a lot is already spent on heat: with a $100/yr heat bill there is no case, at $100k/mo industrial heat bills there is [#1168 · 2024-09-17 · Jonathan Y] [#1169 · 2024-09-17 · Jonathan Y] [#1170 · 2024-09-17 · Jonathan Y]. Target anyone heating with electricity or propane, and don't try to convince people [#4581 · 2025-03-19 · Cade] [#4592 · 2025-03-19 · Cade].
- **Uptime beats novelty.** Downtime hurts hashrate heating, so industrial and business clients that use heat year-round are preferred [#387 · 2024-08-04 · Tyler Stevens] [#137 · 2024-08-02 · Tyler Stevens]. Commercial and industrial sites also benefit from tax write-offs and the ability to run all the time [#3532 · 2025-01-16 · The schnauze].
- **Low-grade industrial heat is a hard sell.** Below-150C steam is cheap and usually already recovered by economizers, so plant integration is difficult; niches with no excess heat are better targets [#769 · 2024-08-23 · Harrison The Space]. Mega-miners struggle with heat reuse because their sites are rural and nobody nearby wants the heat [#767 · 2024-08-23 · Tyler Stevens].
- **Batch loads need buffers.** For microbreweries, a few thousand watts continuous outstrips distribution, and batch processes need buffer tanks [#768 · 2024-08-23 · Harrison The Space]. A biofuel plant that needs heat only 16 of 24 hours can pick the cheapest hours in the daily power market [#5168 · 2025-03-30 · csh2000].
- **Floor space is cheap to trade.** Miners need little floor area: roughly 400 sq ft of space to heat like 10,000 sq ft [#4433 · 2025-03-06 · Travis Bitkle] [#4434 · 2025-03-06 · Travis Bitkle].
- **Every system needs somewhere for heat to go.** Cade's rule is that every system always needs a heat dump (e.g. a greenhouse with a roof exhaust fan) [#3565 · 2025-01-16 · Cade]; others argue dumping only pays in very cheap-power regions (see [Hashrate Heating Economics](../economics/hashrate-heating-economics.md)) [#3564 · 2025-01-16 · Cody Harris].

## Products and vendors

### Whatsminer M64 hydro boilers (Heat Core, RY3T)

The single-phase Whatsminer M64 hydro became the default engine for packaged boilers; see [Whatsminer M64 Hydro Heaters](../hardware/whatsminer-m64-hydro-heaters.md) for the miner itself.

- **Heat Core HS05.** A single-miner "hydro rack" with a rear heat exchanger for hydronic loads such as radiant floor, pool or hot tub [#7896 · 2025-11-23 · Heatpunk Forum]. Spec summary: Manufacturer Heat Core, Model HS05, Miner Compatibility M64, Power "Single Phase. 30A, 220V" [#6974 · 2025-08-11 · Heatpunk Forum]. It ships with a dry cooler in series, 50ft extension tubes and wiring, requiring no customization [#7165 · 2025-08-23 · Tyler Stevens].
- **Heat Core pricing (as of 2025).** HeatCore 5kW AIO hydro units cited at "like $6000" in March 2025 [#4807 · 2025-03-23 · Cody Harris]; at Bitcoin 2025 the Heat Core unit was quoted at $1500 with a radiator attached, versus another boiler retailing for $9,000 [#6316 · 2025-05-31 · Elijah Sanders].
- **Heat Core NS200.** A first NS200 system with a WhatsMiner M65S was commissioned on ~35% propylene glycol + demineralized water (February 2026). Whatsminer's documentation asks for an inhibitor from Peric dosed in wt% without a clear product name or EU source, and a heating distributor did not recognize the HeatCore boiler brand [#9089 · 2026-02-11 · Heatpunk Forum] [#9133 · 2026-02-12 · Deleted Account] [#9134 · 2026-02-12 · Deleted Account].
- **Heat Core and the M74 (March 2026).** Per Heat Core, the M74 is ~240 TH at 3500 Watts and ~325 TH at 5000 Watts with the same form factor and firmware as the 64; Heat Core quoted an MOQ of 60 and would not give a spec sheet [#9527 · 2026-03-05 · Tyler Stevens] [#9528 · 2026-03-05 · Tyler Stevens]. Exergy was told the M74 fits the HS05 but treats this as unconfirmed [#9627 · 2026-03-12 · Mike Clear] [#9632 · 2026-03-12 · Travis Bitkle].
- **RY3T (Switzerland).** A water-based immersion heating system for boilers, floor heating, radiators, tap water and pools [#423 · 2024-08-05 · Christian Naef]. The case holds 1 to 2 Whatsminers of the M56 to M66 series (7-14kw), with control unit and electronics inside the case but outside the tank, and integrates with an existing gas heating system [#1242 · 2024-10-01 · Christian Naef] [#2467 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef] [#2468 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef] [#2459 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef] [#2460 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef]. A kill switch that shuts down everything is there by regulation, and the controller shuts off before miners overheat [#1242 · 2024-10-01 · Christian Naef] [#1247 · 2024-10-01 · Christian Naef].
- **RY3T timeline.** First customer units installing in November 2024 [#1225 · 2024-09-29 · Christian Naef]; presented to 1200 SME entrepreneurs in Switzerland [#1722 · 2024-10-28 · Christian Naef]; by December 2024 another 7 customer units were planned within 3-6 weeks plus 30 pre-orders from Switzerland, Austria and Germany [#2460 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef] [#2462 · 2024-12-04 · Christian Naef]. The RY3T MINI was built with Winchain (RY3T bringing heating expertise, Winchain the mining infrastructure), and the first one in the US was driven from Las Vegas to Denver to heat The Space [#6336 · 2025-06-01 · Christian Naef] [#6294 · 2025-05-31 · Christian Naef]. Positioning: works out of the box, warranty, installed by professional plumbers, owner chooses their own pool, uses surplus solar [#6334 · 2025-06-01 · Christian Naef].
- **Wiring.** Both the RY3T Mini and HS05 are "30 Amp, 220 V" and were wired with a standard L6-30 receptacle [#6970 · 2025-08-11 · Heatpunk Forum].

> **Status: Disputed**
> Is a packaged unit with a bolted-on radiator/dry cooler a heating product? Elijah Sanders criticized the Heat Core unit's extra power source and attached radiator (the machine must go outside) [#6318 · 2025-05-31 · Elijah Sanders]; Christian Naef (RY3T) said a near-identical unit tested was a mining unit, not a heating product, with pipes and heat exchanger far too small [#6334 · 2025-06-01 · Christian Naef]; Dane O argued the heat dump is useful in some localities but should be optional/bypassable [#6324 · 2025-05-31 · Dane O]. An earlier call (flagged as uncertain due to a language barrier) suggested HeatCore products require a dry cooler, which Tyler argued makes residential marketing misleading [#5052 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens] [#5057 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens]. In field use the HS05's dry cooler proved handy for dumping summer solar heat [#7165 · 2025-08-23 · Tyler Stevens]. Best assessment: the in-series dry cooler is a feature where heat demand is seasonal, but buyers should confirm it can be bypassed for heat-first installs.

### Water-heater products

- **Superheat H1.** An electric water heater with integrated bitcoin mining whose final prototype debuted at CES 2026 [#9071 · 2026-02-09 · Heatpunk Forum]. Superheat wrote its own firmware that sets clock speed from water temperature for closed-volume tanks [#8609 · 2026-01-08 · Tyler Stevens] [#8611 · 2026-01-08 · Andrew Geng]. - **Aqueon (Dane O, June 2026).** A domestic hot water heater retrofit for m54-64-74 series miners with a custom controller and 2 heat exchangers: DHW primary, furnace/radiant/heat dump secondary, web UI hosted locally, 5 units targeted for heating season [#10283 · 2026-06-24 · Dane O]. ### Installer-operator business models

- **Ecobit (Quebec).** A small industrial miner (> 1mw) that sells used rigs for business and residential heating [#2308 · 2024-12-01 · The schnauze] [#2613 · 2024-12-08 · The schnauze]. Model: debt-finance miners for industrial warehouse heating; the client gets the heat, Ecobit keeps the mined btc minus the heating expense and ends up with fully depreciated hardware to resell. Montreal power was 6.8 cents / kWh (CAD) and the latest install was reportedly 50 S21s [#2194 · 2024-11-29 · Toine Heat Reuse] [#2195 · 2024-11-29 · Toine Heat Reuse] [#2198 · 2024-11-29 · Toine Heat Reuse]. Used S19 J Pro (104)TH units were offered at $520 / unit (cleaned, 1yr warranty) in November 2024 [#2146 · 2024-11-24 · Toine Heat Reuse]. Their S19s have been solid, some running caked in ice, with higher early failures in fans, ECUs and PSUs [#2335 · 2024-12-01 · The schnauze].
- **Leasing.** One firm leases machines for free and takes a revenue split, because even a $1000 machine is a hard sell [#3551 · 2025-01-16 · The schnauze]. Other ideas: a host rebate (host keeps none of the hashrate but gets a $50 monthly rebate) [#7238 · 2025-09-02 · Elijah Sanders]; a P2P hosting marketplace matching miners with homes and nonprofits that need heat [#7240 · 2025-09-02 · Michael | Bitstorian]; a trailer-mounted C2 splitting pool heating costs 50/50 [#4602 · 2025-03-19 · Dev 🇳🇿].
- **Big miners.** MARA and other large miners started heat-reuse projects; their relative share is expected to shrink as heat reuse grows [#3510 · 2025-01-16 · Michael Schmid @Schnitzel] [#3501 · 2025-01-16 · Michael Schmid @Schnitzel].

## Certification, insurance and permits

- **Insurability is the first question.** Commercial clients pretty much all ask about insurability because miners lack UL/CE certification [#7101 · 2025-08-18 · Tyler Stevens] [#7100 · 2025-08-18 · Heatpunk Forum]. A Canadian heat-reuse firm reported being debanked and refused insurance for being crypto-affiliated [#3060 · 2025-01-03 · The schnauze].
- **The appliance argument.** Per Zack (Loki Boards), relayed by Jon hashing2heating, UL usually doesn't matter until you make 10,000 units; if the PSUs and pumps are UL rated and the ASICs have a shutdown temperature, the system resembles a water heater or electric boiler, an extra appliance inside an already-insured building [#7111 · 2025-08-19 · Jon hashing2heating]. Heatbit used designed-in-accordance-with UL 1042 wording rather than a UL rating; offering warranties makes UL worth considering [#7113 · 2025-08-19 · Jon hashing2heating].
- **Forced air vs immersion.** Building code requires forced air heaters to be listed, and inspectors invoke UL for them; code has no provision for immersion heating through a brazed plate HX [#5566 · 2025-04-16 · Cade] [#7258 · 2025-09-03 · Cade]. Softwarm's route: immersion miners (fluid removes computer fire risk, gear can sit outside and heat is moved in by fluid) plus an external listed PDU on 30 or 50A outlets, and they pass inspections [#7258 · 2025-09-03 · Cade] [#7259 · 2025-09-03 · Cade]. Keeping the PDU external avoids passing power through the tank, which complicates UL ratings and cord compatibility [#3759 · 2025-01-22 · Cade] [#3760 · 2025-01-22 · Cade]. Standard-thread equipment keeps customer systems compliant, with no Frankenstein projects [#6657 · 2025-06-29 · Cade].
- **Local variance.** Idaho's inspector said the group was the only one in the state doing this [#5566 · 2025-04-16 · Cade]; Idaho inspectors may differ from other places [#6428 · 2025-06-08 · Travis Bitkle]. State regs cover snow-melt systems but not concrete slabs as heat exchangers for liquid-cooled servers [#5567 · 2025-04-16 · Travis Bitkle]. In Minnesota a pool heater install needs both a mechanical and an electrical license [#7236 · 2025-09-02 · Elijah Sanders].
- **Pursuing certification.** Elijah Sanders pursued a US safety certification for the M64 in 2025 [#7236 · 2025-09-02 · Elijah Sanders], then held off on a UL rating for the M64S in February 2026: a decline would leave no path to improve, and it would put the M64 on the radar [#9178 · 2026-02-15 · Elijah Sanders] [#9181 · 2026-02-15 · Elijah Sanders].
- **Fusing.** A fuse or breaker in the contactor box is a requirement for UL/CSA approval [#1525 · 2024-10-12 · Toine Heat Reuse].
- **Air quality.** A summit panel claim that hot air-cooled miners release chemicals was called fud: expect some first-run off-gassing like any PCB product, the S17 had an issue but S19 VOC testing came back good, and a post-hashboard filter helps if concerned [#4193 · 2025-02-23 · Patrick Patel] [#4203 · 2025-02-23 · Bob] [#4207 · 2025-02-23 · Toine Heat Reuse] [#4209 · 2025-02-23 · Toine Heat Reuse].

## Adoption barriers

- **Trust and survival.** Heating equals survival in the north, so people trust established HVAC brands over one-off systems; pool heating is a lower-risk entry point [#584 · 2024-08-10 · Bob].
- **Supply chain and trades.** Purpose-built hardware needs chips sold directly, and HVAC/plumbing/electrical tradesmen need education to service heaters [#594 · 2024-08-10 · Tyler Stevens]. Builders asked chipmakers to sell B2B so boards can come in any shape and cooling method, with higher thermal range chips (110-120°) opening more use cases [#1951 · 2024-11-15 · Tyler Stevens] [#1944 · 2024-11-15 · Trevor Bello].
- **Lifetime mismatch.** A water heater lasts ~20 years while a hash board won't, so loops must be modular and upgradable [#1028 · 2024-09-04 · Jonathan Y] [#1030 · 2024-09-04 · Jonathan Y]. Immersion is argued to standardize better, since central or district immersion lets you swap miner generations instead of being tied to one hydro form factor [#8207 · 2025-12-08 · Cade] [#8388 · 2025-12-22 · Dane O].
- **Price anchors.** A miner space heater is a tough sell when baseboard heaters cost about 100 bucks [#3148 · 2025-01-07 · Trevor Bello]. Purpose-built home heaters mostly excite people who could already retrofit an s19 themselves [#4385 · 2025-03-03 · Tyler Stevens], though buyers who can't be bothered may want a SHA-256 unit with decent efficiency (17.7J/th) on a domestic outlet [#4386 · 2025-03-03 · Dev 🇳🇿]. The easiest sale is to a customer about to spend 10 grand on a furnace or boiler anyway [#6658 · 2025-06-29 · Tyler Stevens].
- **Bitcoin onboarding.** Customers bottleneck on explaining mining, wallet setup and cashing out [#2507 · 2024-12-05 · The schnauze] [#2510 · 2024-12-05 · The schnauze]. Common misconceptions: mining generates electricity, or more heat makes more bitcoin [#6934 · 2025-08-06 · Cade]; common pushback: heat pumps, Bitcoin isn't real, profitability skepticism, GPUs [#7218 · 2025-08-28 · Cade].
- **Complexity.** Home Assistant adds complexity that makes deployment in non-technical homes harder [#1371 · 2024-10-08 · Zack Bomsta].
- **Sales ideas.** A test-drive heater plugged in for a month shows electric cost versus mined revenue [#538 · 2024-08-10 · Toine Heat Reuse]; a furnace with a modular ASIC bay ahead of the gas section keeps boards away from furnace heat [#589 · 2024-08-10 · Jonathan Y].

## Scams and safety

- **Too-good prices.** S21 listings at $4/TH are most likely a scam [#1629 · 2024-10-20 · Alex HeatBit]; Alibaba lists impossible prices to engage sales, while AliExpress is more end-user focused [#1619 · 2024-10-20 · Jonathan Y] [#1626 · 2024-10-20 · Jonathan Y].
- **Lookalike firmware sites.** Multiple Vnish-branded sites and X accounts exist; verify toolkit hashes before flashing [#1444 · 2024-10-09 · Jim ⚡️] [#1446 · 2024-10-09 · Jim ⚡️].
- **Compromised accounts (2026).** Hijacked Telegram accounts of known members asked people to schedule a meeting or run code in a terminal; block and verify out-of-band, don't click meeting links, don't take Telegram calls, and never run commands someone sends you [#8899 · 2026-01-28 · PizzAndy] [#9131 · 2026-02-12 · Ian] [#9221 · 2026-02-19 · Dylan Seib] [#9376 · 2026-02-25 · ₿ Minion].
- **Equipment sellers.** Be wary of lurkers who suddenly offer gear and push for sats upfront (S9 cooling plates in one case); build reputation first and use Nostr to verify identity [#10483 · 2026-08-25 · Karl] [#10493 · 2026-08-25 · Scott Marmoll].
- **Unattractive offers.** A Michigan flare-gas offer (1 megawatt site, S21 or better, 50/50 revenue split, $0.00 electricity, $2,000 monthly maintenance, $50,000 commitment fee, 5-year contract) was judged not compelling versus heat reuse [#6474 · 2025-06-15 · Shawn Flowers].
