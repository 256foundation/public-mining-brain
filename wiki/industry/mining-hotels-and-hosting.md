## Overview

A "mining hotel" hosts privately-owned miners in a purpose-built facility — power, cooling, network, and maintenance handled for a fee while the owner keeps custody and (in the honest model) 100% of payouts. For the heat-reuse community it is the seasonal complement to home heating: run hardware in the house when heat is wanted, hotel it when it is not, or hotel permanently when home electrical capacity, noise, or space forbids.

## The landscape (As-Of 2026-10-11)

- **Abundant Mines** (Oregon) — "Own the miners, keep 100% of your bitcoin. Full-service hosting at our hydro-powered Oregon facilities. We handle setup, maintenance, and daily operations." — [capture](../../raw/industry/abundant-mines/2026-10-11-abundant-mines-home.md). Hydro power + full-service is the template: custody stays with the miner, ops go to the hotel.
- **Hotmine "miner hotel"** — the Ukrainian pioneer runs "Готель для майнерів" (a hotel for miners) as a store service alongside its heater SKUs — [capture](../../raw/industry/hotmine/2026-10-11-hotmine-home.md).
- **Freedomtech.hosting** — community-built managed model, described by its founder: "The idea of Freedomtech is thats its a combination model of Voltage (everything in the cloud, nothing local, managed for you) and Umbrel/start9 (nothing in the cloud, all local and you are by yourself)." [#970 · 2024-05-19 · Michael Schmid @Schnitzel] — i.e., hosted infrastructure with sovereignty retained.
- **Lincoin** — named by a pleb planning to host Whatsminers "around August once their new pod is up" [#735 · 2024-04-06 · Expatriotic] — community member comparing hosting options by payout-tooling fit.

## What hosting changes (community view)

- The self-hosting objection is technical, not custody: "The argument against self hosted solo mining has always been the worry of stale blocks. But a cloud service could prolly sort out the peering for you." [#969 · 2024-05-19 · Skot Bitaxe] — well-run hotels are peers, not landlords.
- The heat-reuse trade: hosting removes the heat from where you live. Winter argument is home-side ([Air-Cooled Hashrate Heating](../hardware/air-cooled-hashrate-heating.md)); summer or noise-constrained homes push to hotels. No dated anchor quantifies the seasonal hand-off yet — Phase B candidate.
