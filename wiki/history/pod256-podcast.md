## Overview

POD256 is the 256 Foundation's podcast: in its own words, a Bitcoin podcast "focused on open-source Bitcoin mining, energy, and freedom tech", recorded weekly at Bitcoin Park in Nashville, TN and co-hosted (as of the 2026-10-07 capture) by @econoalchemist, @skot9000 and @tylerkstevens ([pod256.org](../../raw/history/pod256/pod256-org-home.md)). Episode 001 was published 2022-12-03 ([ep 001](../../raw/history/pod256/2022-12-03-pod256-001-hashrate-down-14-from-ath-hashprice-at-atl-territ.md)) and the latest captured, episode 127, on 2026-09-28 ([ep 127](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-09-28-pod256-127-self-hosted-ai-and-open-mining-tools-for-a-more-s.md)). It began as a weekly mining-industry news show with a fixed "State of the Network" segment, and over 2024–2025 turned into the public progress log of the 256 Foundation's open mining stack (Ember One, Libre Board, Mujina, Hydrapool) and of the Heatpunk hashrate-heating scene. For a newcomer, it is the best single chronological record of how the open-source home-mining movement formed; for a builder, the episode index below says where each hardware, firmware and pool milestone was first discussed. The network figures the hosts read on air are collected separately in [Network State 2022–2026](../industry/network-state-2022-2026.md).

## Format and how it changed

1. **Guest interviews (001–003).** One guest (Colin Harper of Luxor, Standard Bitcoin's Tom Masiero and Marty Bent) and a short topic list.
2. **Segment show (004–046).** State of the Network, News, Tangents, Home Miner of the Week and a guest slot called "No Holds Barred" (see e.g. [ep 009](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-02-25-pod256-009-state-of-the-network-news-tangents-home-miner-of.md)). The notes give the block height and a halving countdown and link the same data sources every week: Bitrawr's difficulty estimator, mempool.space, Luxor's Hashrate Index and Braiins Insights ([ep 005](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-01-27-pod256-005-expected-difficulty-adjustment-trending-miner-pri.md)); Newhedge replaced Bitrawr by episode 030 ([ep 030](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-10-07-pod256-030-no-holds-barred-w-special-guest-skot-instigator-b.md)). The last full snapshot is episode 046 ([ep 046](../../raw/history/pod256/2024-05-24-pod256-046-no-holds-barred-w-special-guest-luke-dashjr-chair.md)).
3. **Prose summaries (047 onward).** Guests shift toward pool and hardware builders (Wilson Mining, boerst, Bob McElrath).
4. **Keywords, takeaways and chapters (roughly 061–080)**, e.g. [ep 080](../../raw/history/pod256/2025-08-01-pod256-080-the-chilling-effect-legal-risks-in-privacy-softwa.md).
5. **Project log (081 onward).** Dense first-person paragraphs about the open stack, heat reuse and freedom tech; from 2026 several episodes are recorded live and announced on the 256 Foundation forum, e.g. episode 114 at 2100 UTC on Wednesday, May 13th ([forum: Ep 114](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-05-12-forum-pod256-episode-114.md)).

Episode 100 marked three years of POD256 and recalled the early "Hash Cast" days ([ep 100](../../raw/history/pod256/2025-12-31-pod256-100-dismantling-the-black-box-hydra-pool-libre-board.md)). Its origin story, as retold there: through 2022 Eco hosted a solo video podcast, the Hashcast, released roughly every difficulty adjustment (about every two weeks); when he moved to shut it down at the end of 2022, Rod — its biggest fan — offered to take on 80 to 85% of the production, and the format (state of the network, news, guests) carried straight into POD256, which started in January 2023 and took three years to reach 100 episodes ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)). Scott became a regular in early-to-mid 2023, once the Bitaxe "started being a thing that actually kinda worked", and Rod recalled Eric of Gridless Compute announcing he would fly from Nairobi, Kenya to the Nashville Energy and Mining Summit in '24 because of the show ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)). Breaks: episode 027 mentions a two week hiatus ([ep 027](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-08-19-pod256-027-state-of-the-network-tangents-no-holds-barred-w-s.md)) and episode 125 opens "After a short hiatus" ([ep 125](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-09-08-pod256-125-why-open-source-and-ai-are-colliding-in-bitcoin-m.md)). Episode 126 notes that Eco called it #125 on air by mistake ([ep 126](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-09-22-pod256-126-why-open-source-bitcoin-mining-matters-more-than.md)), and episode 127 opens with an apology for it: "I said it was one twenty five, but it was actually one twenty six" ([POD256 e127](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2026/2026-09-28-e127.md)). In 2025–2026 the show prepped episodes with a dedicated podcast AI agent on BuzzRelay, a Nostr-based collaboration tool ([ep 125](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-09-08-pod256-125-why-open-source-and-ai-are-colliding-in-bitcoin-m.md)).

### Supporting the show (hash-pointing)

- **2023–2024: Lincoin.** "Stream Sats, Point Hash" asked listeners to pick a regional Lincoin stratum URL on port 3333 and use a `pod256.YourAlias` worker name; worker names were read out weekly ([ep 006](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-02-03-pod256-006-state-of-the-network-the-taproot-wizard-and-the-4.md); [ep 037](../../raw/history/pod256/2024-02-24-pod256-037-no-holds-barred-w-special-guest-rapha-zagury-alph.md)). By episode 082 the instructions lived at "256foundation.org/mining-links.html" (Eco fumbled the URL live, then corrected it to `mining_links.html`): Lincoin was kept as the "full pay per share" option with `pod two fifty six` plus a dot-alias worker name — "Whatever crazy thing you want Tyler to read" — and Ocean, Public Pool and Solo CK Pool were listed for listeners who would "use your Bitcoin address as your username", the Foundation's address, so a solo block would pay the Foundation; the last step is restarting the miner ([POD256 e082](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-08-27-e082.md)).
- **2025–2026: migrating hashers to the Foundation's Hydrapool.** When Eco opened the Foundation's Hydrapool instance after the December 2025 release he said he would ask "all the hashers who've been pointing Hashrate to pod two fifty six accounts, like Ocean and CK Pool and LingQoin to start migrating those over" to it ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)); the plan dates to the release episode, where Eco — running the pool on an old Dell Optiplex with about 12 terahash from five workers — said the instance would run the Telehash and then pull in supporters' hash from Ocean, solo CKPool and Public Pool accounts ([POD256 e097](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-03-e097.md)). He was explicit that "It is not the intention of the two fifty six foundation to run a public mining pool" — any block found pays "100% of that is going to go to the two fifty six foundations" ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)). By the post-NEMS debrief the dashboard read "There's four forty one terahash on here", with stragglers still on Lincoin and the Backwater Mining Collective — "Move your hash rate to 256f.org" — and a few on Solo CK, Ocean and Public Pool ([POD256 e102](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2026/2026-01-30-e102.md)).
- **Donations and sponsors.** PayNym donations from episode 008 ([ep 008](../../raw/history/pod256/2023-02-17-pod256-008-state-of-the-network-argo-blockchain-ceo-steps-do.md)); a Tier II Supporters list headed by Foundry USA from episode 052 ([ep 052](../../raw/history/pod256/2024-08-22-pod256-052-from-owls-on-cocaine-to-bitcoin-mining-a-wild-rid.md)).
- **2026: the Foundation's own Hydrapool.** Listeners are asked to point hash at the 256 Foundation pool when the show is live, or to run their own Hydrapool ([ep 104](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-02-11-pod256-104-ai-open-source-bitcoin-mining-and-battling-survei.md)); the HashDash leaderboard at dash.256f.org gained loyalty, luck and difficulty rankings ([ep 114](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-05-13-pod256-114-open-source-wins-in-vegas-100k-boost-doomaxe-demo.md); [ep 126](../../raw/history/pod256/2026-09-22-pod256-126-why-open-source-bitcoin-mining-matters-more-than.md)).

> **Status: Disputed** — which pool found block 881423
> Episode 100 says that during the Telehash "an Apollo-powered solo pool briefly wrangled an exahash and struck block 881423" ([ep 100](../../raw/history/pod256/2025-12-31-pod256-100-dismantling-the-black-box-hydra-pool-libre-board.md)), and the January 2026 newsletter likewise says "#1 with the Apollo solo pool" ([newsletter #13](../../raw/history/256f-newsletter/2026-01-14-assembling-freedom-13.md)). The Foundation's Telehash page says participants point hashrate at the Foundation's Hydrapool instance ([Telehash page](../../raw/history/256f-org/256foundation-org-telehash.md)). Best assessment: the podcast and newsletter accounts are first-hand and specific; the Telehash page describes the format of later events. See [256 Foundation Organization](256-foundation-organization.md).
> The episode 100 transcript adds the mechanics: the pool ran on a Futurebit Apollo running the solo-pool software Futurebit built with Con Kolivas, and "at the height of the event, 1.12 Exahash pointed to that Apollo future bit" produced "Block number 881423" — thought to be the first exahash ever pointed at that software ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)). The box was crash-prone: the hashrate would ramp "50 petahash, 100 petahash, 500 petahash, 800 petahash" and then crash to zero, partly because the config had been edited so the coinbase would say "two fifty six foundation in the Coinbase reward" and a syntax error slipped in ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)). At the moment of the find roughly 800 petahash remained pointed at the solo pool, and "the block was found by a miner that belonged to Megawatt", whose people were in the room that night; the reward was "3.14 Bitcoin or whatever it was" ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)).

### Telehash by the numbers, as recalled on air

The machine transcripts put figures on the later events that the show notes do not:

- **Telehash #1 (Nashville, block 881423).** "at the height of the event, 1.12 Exahash pointed to that Apollo future bit", after ramps of "50 petahash, 100 petahash, 500 petahash, 800 petahash"; the reward was "3.14 Bitcoin or whatever it was" ([POD256 e100](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2025/2025-12-31-e100.md)).

"Around 2,000 of those were Electron" Energy workers; "They supported a full exahash for the full duration of the event" ([POD256 e115](../../raw/history/pod256/transcripts/2026-10-07/transcripts/2026/2026-05-27-e115.md)).
