## Overview

The 256 Foundation's story as its own community lived it in Telegram, February 2024 → October 2026: incorporation through 501(c)(3), the grant program, the hardware and pool projects, the Telehash events, and the slow migration of community infrastructure from Telegram to forum (and experiments beyond). Dates are message-anchored to the [signal digest](../../raw/history/2026-10-08-256f-telegram-signal.md).
