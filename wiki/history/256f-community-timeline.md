## Overview

The 256 Foundation's story as its own community lived it in Telegram, February 2024 → October 2026: incorporation through 501(c)(3), the grant program, the hardware and pool projects, the Telehash events, and the slow migration of community infrastructure from Telegram to forum (and experiments beyond). Dates in the first three sections are message-anchored to the [signal digest](../../raw/history/2026-10-08-256f-telegram-signal.md). The Milestones from org records section adds grant, release, fundraiser and program dates from the Foundation's own website, X account, newsletter and forum. For the organization itself (legal structure, board, programs, funding), see [The 256 Foundation](256-foundation-organization.md).

> **Status: Disputed**: which pool found block 881423
> The Foundation's Telehash page says block 881423 was found on "our Hydrapool instance" ([256F: Telehash](../../raw/history/256f-org/256foundation-org-telehash.md)). The Telegram digest above (2025-01-27) shows the donation endpoint pointed at a self-hosted Apollo ([256F Telegram digest](../../raw/history/2026-10-08-256f-telegram-signal.md)). The Foundation posted on 2025-03-25 that it was "running a @FutureBit Apollo for our node and stratum server" ([X archive: 2025 H1](../../raw/history/256f-research/2026-10-07-x-256foundation-2025-h1.md)). Best assessment: the block was mined through the Apollo solo pool. Hydra Pool's first version was built later, for Telehash #2. Details are in [The 256 Foundation](256-foundation-organization.md).

> **Status: Disputed**: Telehash #4 hashrate
> The Telegram entry above records 1.2 EH/s mid-event. The forum metrics give a 2.49 EH/s peak and a 1.32 EH/s average ([Telehash #4 metrics](../../raw/history/256f-org/2026-05-21-forum-telehash-4-performance-metrics.md)), and the Foundation's X post said "Over 2.5 EH/s" ([X archive: 2026](../../raw/history/256f-research/2026-10-07-x-256foundation-2026.md)). Best assessment: 1.2 EH/s was a point-in-time reading near the average. The forum's computed peak of 2.49 EH/s is authoritative.
