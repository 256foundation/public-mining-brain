## Overview

The Hashrate Heatpunks are a builder community focused on using bitcoin mining hardware as a heat source. The Telegram group's digest begins on 2024-08-02. Over the next two years it registered heatpunks.org, published "A Heatpunk's Manifesto", held the inaugural Heatpunk Summit (Undermine, Denver, February 2025), got its own panel at Bitcoin 2025, launched a forum that now mirrors into the chat, held a second summit in February 2026, and started weekly Home Assistant office hours. Throughout, it overlapped with the 256 Foundation's Telehash fundraisers and open-source stack (Mujina, Hydrapool). The group sees itself as a high-signal chat for system builders, and it keeps to bitcoin: heating with other proof-of-work coins is off-topic [#3454 · 2025-01-15 · Tyler Stevens] [#5032 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens]. This page lists dated milestones. For the technical lessons, start with the articles under See Also.

## What carried over (for newcomers)

- **Pin the answers.** From 2024-08-02, key solution messages were pinned so new members could scroll back to them [#270 · 2024-08-02 · Tyler Stevens]. In 2025 a forum took over this role, because chat history is hard to search [#4936 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens] [#5112 · 2025-03-29 · Cody Harris].
- **Meet in person and record it.** Each summit focused on builders and tradespeople (electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs) and published recordings afterward [#2703 · 2024-12-12 · Tyler Stevens] [#5258 · 2025-04-02 · Trevor Bello] [#9526 · 2026-03-05 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **Heat is the product.** The founding framing was "Heat IS the product.  BTC is the prize": heaters that come with mini lotto tickets [#985 · 2024-09-04 · Jonathan Y] [#983 · 2024-09-04 · Tyler Stevens].

## 2024: founding and first public footprint

- **2024-08-02**: earliest digest messages. Early members' first projects included an immersion pool heater and replacing a 3000 W electric water heater with one immersion miner for hydronic floor heat [#65 · 2024-08-02 · Gerald Glickman] [#135 · 2024-08-02 · Tyler Stevens]. Key solution messages were pinned for new members [#270 · 2024-08-02 · Tyler Stevens]. An older S9 water block Discord predates the group [#5095 · 2025-03-28 · Dev 🇳🇿].
- **August 2024 – Luxor contact.** David Urlacher offered to pass heat-reuse firmware requests on to Luxor [#528 · 2024-08-09 · Tyler Stevens].
- **August 2024 – Manifesto.** Tyler Stevens shared his essay "A Heatpunk's Manifesto", inspired by "A Cypherpunk's Manifesto". It argues that hashrate heating will hyperdecentralize mining. A Braiins blog post on heat-optimized ASICs accompanied it [#533 · 2024-08-10 · Tyler Stevens] [#532 · 2024-08-10 · Tyler Stevens].
- **August 2024 – Book underway.** Tyler's book on hashrate heat reuse was in progress. Its first chapter is "Heat is a Product, Not a Problem", and chapter 4 compares residential and small business, industrial, and mega miners and public companies [#716 · 2024-08-22 · Tyler Stevens] [#767 · 2024-08-23 · Tyler Stevens].
- **2024-08-23 – heatpunks.org registered.** Plans covered a forum, a shared blog, fundamentals (ASICs, HVAC, hydronic, control) and supplier links [#755 · 2024-08-23 · Tyler Stevens] [#758 · 2024-08-23 · Tyler Stevens]. Members suggested sections on commercial and industrial use cases such as chocolate makers, hotels and microbreweries [#759 · 2024-08-23 · Nicolas Drouin-Audet].
- **September 2024 – Press.** A Time article covered heat reuse at a Bitcoin bathhouse [#1100 · 2024-09-12 · Jonathan Y].
- **September 2024 – 256 Foundation tie-in.** A monthly mining meetup at Bitcoin Park was announced. The 256 Foundation's first annual fundraiser, The Telehash, was set for January 29, 2025, alongside the Nashville Energy & Mining Summit [#1137 · 2024-09-15 · econoalchemist].
- **September 2024 – First panel.** A pleb mining panel ran at the mining conference in Brainerd. The Build-a-Mine Pod covered heatpunk topics [#1220 · 2024-09-28 · Trevor Bello].
- **2024-09-29 – DATUM.** OCEAN's DATUM was still in private beta, available by emailing OCEAN [#1231 · 2024-09-29 · Tyler Stevens]. It later became the community's default way to build its own block templates (see [Pool Choice for Heat Miners](../pools/pool-choice-for-heat-miners.md)).
- **October 2024 – European builders.** Attakaï from France introduced its S9-era heater work, which it started in late 2022 [#1312 · 2024-10-08 · Jim ⚡️].
- **October 2024 – District heating.** A Norway site linked to Hashlabs was reported to send heat to a small community's district heating [#1471 · 2024-10-11 · Trevor Bello].
- **November 2024 – Podcast.** Trevor Bello's podcast featured Tyler Stevens on hashrate heat and the Heatpunk Manifesto [#1940 · 2024-11-14 · Trevor Bello] [#2112 · 2024-11-21 · csh2000].
- **December 2024 – Media and new members.** Hashrate heating was due to be discussed with Bitcoin Magazine [#2435 · 2024-12-03 · Tyler Stevens]. Ecobit (Quebec) joined after meeting Toine and seeing Tyler's talk at Braiins [#2308 · 2024-12-01 · The schnauze]. A European home and pleb miner conference was organizing [#2169 · 2024-11-26 · Jim ⚡️].
- **2024-12-12 – Summit announced.** The plan was a 1.5 day summit devoted entirely to hashrate heating, held at The Space in Denver and aimed at contractors and tradespeople, with recorded talks and panels [#2703 · 2024-12-12 · Tyler Stevens] [#2704 · 2024-12-12 · Tyler Stevens]. It was named Undermine, co-planned with Cody Harris, and set for Feb14. Bitcoin Mechanic of OCEAN was announced as a speaker [#2709 · 2024-12-13 · Nicolas Drouin-Audet] [#2914 · 2024-12-23 · Tyler Stevens] [#2965 · 2024-12-29 · Tyler Stevens].
- **December 2024 – Bitcoin 2025 slot.** Hashrate heating was given its own panel at Bitcoin 2025 [#2833 · 2024-12-19 · Tyler Stevens] [#2825 · 2024-12-19 · Tyler Stevens].

## 2025: summits, forum and the main stage

- **January 2025**: Undermine was billed as "the 2025 Heatpunk summit", and attendees were promised copies of Tyler's upcoming heat-reuse book [#3099 · 2025-01-05 · Tyler Stevens] [#3033 · 2025-01-03 · Tyler Stevens]. A building-automation and HVAC attendee from Long confirmed [#3103 · 2025-01-05 · Tyler Stevens]. The group restated its scope as bitcoin hashrate heating only [#3454 · 2025-01-15 · Tyler Stevens].
- **February 2025 – Stackin Sats.** Trevor Bello launched "Stackin Sats", a show about building hashrate-heating and open-source projects that succeeded Build-a-Mine [#3841 · 2025-02-02 · Trevor Bello] [#3839 · 2025-02-02 · Trevor Bello].
- **February 2025 – Hashlabs joins.** Hashlabs CEO Jaran Mellerud joined and described Nordic district-heating sites [#4068 · 2025-02-14 · Jaran Mellerud]. He later hosted a heat-reuse X Space on district heating and industrial uses [#4272 · 2025-02-26 · Jaran Mellerud].
- **February 2025 – Undermine at The Space.** A solo-mining Hashrate Heatpunks DATUM gateway was set up for the event next to The Space's pooled gateway, and mechanic led a DATUM walkthrough in the workshop time [#4085 · 2025-02-15 · Dylan Seib] [#4094 · 2025-02-15 · Dylan Seib] [#4010 · 2025-02-12 · Cody Harris]. The Space had installed 19kw of solar [#4104 · 2025-02-15 · Tyler Stevens]. Afterwards Tyler thanked attendees of "a fantastic innaugural heatpunk summit" [#4220 · 2025-02-24 · Tyler Stevens]. A panel claim that air-cooled miners emit harmful chemicals was later disputed in the chat [#4193 · 2025-02-23 · Patrick Patel] [#4207 · 2025-02-23 · Toine Heat Reuse].
- **March 2025 – Forum vs chat.** Members debated invite-only chat, which stays focused but may exclude builders, against a searchable forum for hydronics, HVAC, control and sizing threads. Some worried that forums die, and Telegram Topics was also suggested [#4936 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens] [#4948 · 2025-03-28 · Mark | @satstackingpleb] [#4951 · 2025-03-28 · Michael Schmid @Schnitzel] [#5115 · 2025-03-29 · Scott Offord]. The X community was renamed Proof-Of-Heat and kept separate from the Telegram, which stays for system builders [#5017 · 2025-03-28 · Scott Offord] [#5032 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens]. ALT bitdevs planned a month-long virtual hackathon in June on decentralizing mining that would feature Heatpunk builders [#4529 · 2025-03-14 · Will Ramirez].
- **April 2025 – Summit video and Mujina.** The Heatpunks summit video was published [#5258 · 2025-04-02 · Trevor Bello]. The lead of the 256 Foundation's Mujina firmware joined to gather heater feature requests [#5409 · 2025-04-10 · Tyler Stevens] [#5410 · 2025-04-10 · Ryan]. The Space's DATUM gateway, where many Heatpunks point hashrate, grew from 16-18 PH/s on 2025-04-10 to over 20PH/s on 2025-04-15, and peaked at 27PH/s on 2025-04-28 [#5417 · 2025-04-10 · Dylan Seib] [#5526 · 2025-04-15 · Dylan Seib] [#5843 · 2025-04-28 · Dylan Seib].
- **April 2025 – Forum launch.** The Hashrate Heatpunk Forum was teased to go live the following week, and members were signing up on the heatpunks site by 2025-04-24 [#5634 · 2025-04-18 · Tyler Stevens] [#5767 · 2025-04-24 · R D].
- **May 2025 – Bitcoin 2025 (Las Vegas).** Five heatpunks spoke on a panel on Thursday the 29th at 11:40 AM, and a Heatpunk meetup took place in the Mining Lounge [#6112 · 2025-05-24 · Tyler Stevens] [#6211 · 2025-05-28 · Tyler Stevens]. The first RY3T MINI in the US was driven from Las Vegas to Denver to heat The Space [#6294 · 2025-05-31 · Christian Naef]. The Space's DATUM node offered 1% fees at stratum+tcp://datum.denver.space:3333 [#6276 · 2025-05-30 · Travis Bitkle]. Summit hot tubs appeared in a Guy Swann video [#6288 · 2025-05-31 · Tom Taber].
- **2025-06-03 / 2025-06-04 – Forum mirroring.** heatpunks.org went down for server maintenance and came back the next day. From 2025-06-04, new forum topics are mirrored into the Telegram chat [#6360 · 2025-06-03 · Tyler Stevens] [#6367 · 2025-06-04 · Tyler Stevens] [#6381 · 2025-06-04 · Tyler Stevens].
- **June 2025 – Exergy test site.** Exergy and The Space received two M64-based boilers, the RY3T Mini and the Heat Core HS05, to test in a radiant floor system [#6446 · 2025-06-10 · Heatpunk Forum]. A public TIDES vs FPPS payout experiment began on 2025.06.02 [#6448 · 2025-06-10 · Heatpunk Forum]. Tyler posted a walkthrough of chapter 3 of his book, "Bitcoin Mining Heat Reuse" ("What Makes an Ideal Candidate") [#6453 · 2025-06-12 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **July 2025 – Outreach.** Congressman Nick Begich of Alaska watched the Heatpunk panel [#6728 · 2025-07-07 · Tyler Stevens]. Travis's water heater project was encouraged for ATL BitLab's "Battle of The Mine Hackers" hackathon [#6683 · 2025-07-02 · Tyler Stevens]. Tyler's book was called the most popular Bitcoin book on campus at the largest US campus Bitcoin program [#6798 · 2025-07-17 · Michael | Bitstorian]. A hashrate heating pitch to Juneau, Alaska residents was published [#6805 · 2025-07-18 · Tyler Stevens].
- **August 2025 – Build files and the book.** The Exergy radiant floor install at The Space was finished with hired plumbers [#6973 · 2025-08-11 · Heatpunk Forum]. The book was offered as a free pdf download [#7099 · 2025-08-18 · Heatpunk Forum]. StealthMiner, Slim19/21 and air-baffle 3D files were posted to heatpunks.org [#7203 · 2025-08-28 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **September 2025 – Chips arrive.** PizzAndy posted that BMZ2 chips arriving was a big day [#7317 · 2025-09-09 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **October–November 2025 – Second summit set.** Members were planning for the next summit in February [#7388 · 2025-10-06 · Trevor Bello]. CNBC was reportedly working on a heatpunks story after a Forbes article [#7694 · 2025-11-14 · Trevor Bello]. A forum thread documenting the Whatsminer API was started [#7885 · 2025-11-23 · Tyler Stevens]. A Mujina developer looked for collaborators to demo the full 256 Foundation open-source stack as a sous vide cooker [#8031 · 2025-11-30 · Heatpunk Forum].

## 2026: second summit, office hours, Telehash

- **January 2026 – Telehash #3.** The 256 Foundation fundraiser started Wednesday, Jan 21 at 1 pm Central. The aim was to solo mine a block live during an 8 hour livestream, funding grants for Mujina Firmware, Ember One Hashboard, Libre Control Board and Hydrapool (dash.256f.org) [#8675 · 2026-01-15 · Tyler Stevens] [#8767 · 2026-01-21 · Tyler Stevens]. It ran longer thanks to donated hashrate from Elektron Energy and "1EH NiceHash" [#8801 · 2026-01-22 · Tyler Stevens]. The 256 Foundation's Hydrapool instance accepts any username, including a nostr address linked to a profile [#8677 · 2026-01-15 · Tyler Stevens].
- **January 2026 – Forum moves.** The forum moved to forum.heatpunks.org URLs by late January [#8976 · 2026-01-30 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **February 2026 – Second Heatpunk Summit (Denver).** Held at the end of February 2026 [#9016 · 2026-02-03 · Reckless Apotheosis] [#9071 · 2026-02-09 · Heatpunk Forum]. Superheat introduced its H1 water heater on the forum in the run-up [#9071 · 2026-02-09 · Heatpunk Forum]. A hashrate heatpunks nostr profile was created as a shared miner username, so members can donate hashrate to the 256 Foundation as a team on Hash Dash [#9277 · 2026-02-21 · Tyler Stevens] [#9896 · 2026-04-20 · Tyler Stevens].
- **March 2026 – Recordings and office hours.** Summit recordings were published as a YouTube playlist on the forum [#9526 · 2026-03-05 · Heatpunk Forum]. Education resources were collected at https://heatpunks.org/education [#9618 · 2026-03-10 · Tyler Stevens]. The weekly Exergy Home Assistant office hours began on Wednesdays at 10am Mountain, starting 3/4 (2026-03-04), to help with heater controls and integrations [#9482 · 2026-03-02 · Dylan Seib] [#9933 · 2026-04-22 · Heatpunk Forum]. When The Space's DATUM had problems, Exergy offered stratum+tcp://mine.exergyheat.com:3334 as a backup [#9708 · 2026-03-20 · Dylan Seib] [#9759 · 2026-03-27 · Dylan Seib].
- **May 2026 – TELEHASH#4.** Set for May 19th, 2026 at the Bitcoin Park campus in Austin TX [#9892 · 2026-04-20 · Tyler Stevens].
- **July 2026 – Open-source heater app.** An open-source space-heater app was released at https://github.com/heatpunk/hashboard [#10297 · 2026-07-01 · MΛRCUS].
- **August 2026 – BIP-110 fork.** The chain split reached heaters too. BIP-110 hit mandatory signaling at block 961,632, and its nodes forked off after OCEAN mined a non-signaling block. With less than 3% miner support the BIP-110 chain was left with almost no hashrate [#10435 · 2026-08-08 · Christopher]. One heater owner moved hashrate to Datum Denver during the event [#10423 · 2026-08-08 · ₿ Minion].
- **September 2026 – Next summit announced.** It will focus heavily on workshops for building with open-source mining tools: firmware customization, hashboard mods, control boards and pool setups [#10594 · 2026-09-29 · Tyler Stevens].
