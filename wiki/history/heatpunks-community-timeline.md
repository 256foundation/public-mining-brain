## Overview

The Hashrate Heatpunks are a builder community focused on using bitcoin mining hardware as a heat source. The Telegram group's digest begins on 2024-08-02. Over the next two years it registered heatpunks.org, published "A Heatpunk's Manifesto", held the inaugural Heatpunk Summit (Undermine, Denver, February 2025), got its own panel at Bitcoin 2025, launched a forum that now mirrors into the chat, held a second summit in February 2026, and started weekly Home Assistant office hours. The forum — site record created 2025-03-31 — stood at 119 topics, 682 posts, 138 users and 624 likes by the 2026-10-10 census, with 101 topics publicly listed. Throughout, the community overlapped with the 256 Foundation's Telehash fundraisers and open-source stack (Mujina, Hydrapool). The group sees itself as a high-signal chat for system builders, and it keeps to bitcoin: heating with other proof-of-work coins is off-topic [#3454 · 2025-01-15 · Tyler Stevens] [#5032 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens]. This page lists dated milestones. Telegram-sourced facts keep [#msgid] anchors; forum-sourced facts link their topic captures directly. For the technical lessons, start with the articles under See Also.

## What carried over (for newcomers)

- **Pin the answers.** From 2024-08-02, key solution messages were pinned so new members could scroll back to them [#270 · 2024-08-02 · Tyler Stevens]. In 2025 a forum took over this role, because chat history is hard to search [#4936 · 2025-03-28 · Tyler Stevens] [#5112 · 2025-03-29 · Cody Harris].
- **Meet in person and record it.** Each summit focused on builders and tradespeople (electricians, plumbers, HVAC techs) and published recordings afterward [#2703 · 2024-12-12 · Tyler Stevens] [#5258 · 2025-04-02 · Trevor Bello] [#9526 · 2026-03-05 · Heatpunk Forum].
- **Heat is the product.** The founding framing was "Heat IS the product.  BTC is the prize": heaters that come with mini lotto tickets [#985 · 2024-09-04 · Jonathan Y] [#983 · 2024-09-04 · Tyler Stevens].

- **March 2026 – Recordings and office hours.** Summit recordings were published as a YouTube playlist on the forum — "all publicly available content from the 2026 Heatpunk Summit" [#9526 · 2026-03-05 · Heatpunk Forum] ([2026 Heatpunk Summit Panel & Workshop Recordings](../../raw/history/heatpunks-forum/2026-03-05-forum-2026-heatpunk-summit-panel-workshop-recordings.md)). Education resources were collected at https://heatpunks.org/education [#9618 · 2026-03-10 · Tyler Stevens]. The weekly Exergy Home Assistant office hours began on Wednesdays at 10am Mountain, starting 3/4 (2026-03-04), to help with heater controls and integrations [#9482 · 2026-03-02 · Dylan Seib] [#9933 · 2026-04-22 · Heatpunk Forum]. When The Space's DATUM had problems, Exergy offered stratum+tcp://mine.exergyheat.com:3334 as a backup [#9708 · 2026-03-20 · Dylan Seib] [#9759 · 2026-03-27 · Dylan Seib].
- **May 2026 – TELEHASH#4.** Set for May 19th, 2026 at the Bitcoin Park campus in Austin TX [#9892 · 2026-04-20 · Tyler Stevens].
- **June 2026 – Fornax Heater.** 418Mining introduced the [Fornax Heater](../../raw/history/heatpunks-forum/2026-06-01-forum-introducing-the-fornax-heater-diy-heater-miner-with-spouse-approved-aesthetics.md), a DIY singleboard heater/miner "built on an APW3++ PSU and S19/21 hashboard/control board combo" with 140mm fans — near-silent at heat outputs up to 600w with the recommended Arctic P14 Pro fans — descended from a predecessor shown at UnderMine. The first unit went to The Space as a loaner, and the files went up free on Printables and Cults3D on 2026-06-24.
- **July 2026 – Open-source heater app.** An open-source space-heater app was released at https://github.com/heatpunk/hashboard [#10297 · 2026-07-01 · MΛRCUS].
- **July 2026 – Peer-reviewed heat numbers and the 2027 summit.** Tyler linked a [Scientific Research Paper on Hashrate Heating Featured in Science Direct](../../raw/history/heatpunks-forum/2026-07-21-forum-scientific-research-paper-on-hashrate-heating-featured-in-science-direct.md) (2026-07-21) — research on heating water with an S19 Pro Hydro that Daniel Batten had shared on X back in May. Its finding, in Tyler's reading: only about ~90% of the electricity input to the miner was useful heat at the delivery point, the rest lost to the power supply, control board and chassis radiation — against modern furnaces and boilers at >95% AFUE. The next day the forum opened the [2027 Heatpunk Summit Waitlist Open!](../../raw/history/heatpunks-forum/2026-10-10-forum-heatpunks-census.md) topic (2026-07-22).

With less than 3% miner support the BIP-110 chain was left with almost no hashrate [#10435 · 2026-08-08 · Christopher]. One heater owner moved hashrate to Datum Denver during the event [#10423 · 2026-08-08 · ₿ Minion].
