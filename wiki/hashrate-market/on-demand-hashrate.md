## Overview

Point-in-time price points and mechanics for buying, renting, and donating hashrate — the market side of community mining events like Telehash. All numbers here are As-Of their cited dates; hashrate markets move fast.

## Price points (with dates)

- Braiins Hashpower minimum order: 10k sats (~$9) instantly deploys ~2.3 PH/s for 2 hours; sign up and order in under a minute — used to boost Telehash Nashville (January 2026) [#4280 · 2026-01-20 · C Funk]
- Rigly: lock in rented hashrate in advance to support open-source mining development for Telehash #4 (blockparty.rigly.io/256foundation) [#4790 · 2026-05-14 · Tyler Stevens]

## How Telehash is fueled

- Telehash's fuel is donated hashrate — community members point rigs at the event pool (pool.256foundation.org:3333) rather than paying [#1374 · 2024-10-20 · econoalchemist] [#4168 · 2026-01-07 · econoalchemist]
- Telehash #4 (2026-05-19) peaked at 1.2 EH/s with rented + donated hashrate combined [#4835 · 2026-05-19 · BJ]
- Gamification idea: numbered nostr badges for each Telehash (#1, #2, …) with rank tiers — Sat/Tera/Petahasher — or by millions of shares submitted [#2084 · 2025-05-05 · Reckless Apotheosis]
- Operation Bitcoin events ran public Telehash dashboards (hydrapool.operationbitcoin.io; ob-telehash-dashboard repo) [#5018 · 2026-07-01 · Gabe Lord] [#5048 · 2026-07-17 · Nickamoto]
